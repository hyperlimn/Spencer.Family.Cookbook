3 cups cooked & cubed chicken 1 large onion, chopped 3 cans (12 oz. each) chili beans, undrained 1 can (12 oz.) chopped tomatoes, undrained 1 can (6 oz.) tomato paste 1 cup frozen corn 3 Tbsp. chili powder

Combine all ingredients in slow cooker. Stir. Cover and cook on low for 8 hours.
