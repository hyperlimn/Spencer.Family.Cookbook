  - "Bake at 350 degrees for 30-35 minutes. Let cool"
  - "completely."
  - "8 oz. cream cheese (softened)"
  - "½ cup orange juice"
  - "1 small box vanilla instant pudding"
  - "1/3 cup sugar"
  - "1 (8 oz.) tub frozen, whipped topping, thawed"
  - "Beat together the cream cheese, orange juice, pudding, and"
  - "sugar until creamy. Then fold in an 8-oz. container of"
  - "whipped topping."
  - "Spread over the cooled cake and refrigerate."
  - "Note: If you are unable to find an orange cake mix,"
  - "substitute a white cake mix and add 1 Tbsp. of orange"
  - "extract and a little food coloring."
