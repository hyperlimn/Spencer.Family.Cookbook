ingredients:
  - "½ cup melted butter"
  - "2 lbs. frozen hash browns, Southern style"
  - "½ chopped onion"
  - "2 cups grated Cheddar cheese"
  - "1 tsp. salt"
  - "½ tsp. pepper"
  - "1 can cream of chicken soup"
  - "¾ cup sour cream"
  - "1 cup Corn Flake® crumbs"
---

Mix all ingredients together except butter. Place in a 9-x 13-inch greased pan. Top with butter and 1 cup Corn Flakes® crumbs. Bake at 350º for 40 – 45 minutes.

Michelle’s Variation: Use O’Brien style southern potatoes (frozen). Red and green peppers and onions are included in this.

Very cheesy and tasty!
