ingredients:
  - "1 (13 oz.) can crushed pineapple"
  - "1 (6 oz.) pkg. red Jell-O®"
  - "1 cup ginger ale"
  - "1 lb. can jellied cranberry sauce"
  - "1 small container whipped topping"
  - "1 (8 oz.) cream cheese"
---

Drain pineapple and add enough water to make 2 cups of liquid. Heat and dissolve Jell-O® with this mixture. Let cool. Add ginger ale. Refrigerate until syrupy.

Beat cranberry sauce at low speed. Add pineapple and Jell-O®. Put in 9-x 13-inch dish or mold.

Beat cream cheese and add whipped topping. Spread on Jell-O® after firmly jelled. Add nuts if desired.
