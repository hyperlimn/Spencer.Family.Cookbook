  - "3 Tbsp. sugar"
  - "3¼ cups cold milk, divided"
  - "1 pkg. (4-oz. serving size) Jell-O® Chocolate Instant Pudding"
  - "2 pkg. (4-oz.) serving size each) Jell-O® White Chocolate"
  - "flavor Instant Pudding"
  - "1 ½ cups thawed whipped topping"
  - "1 ½ cups miniature marshmallows"
  - "½ square Baker’s Semisweet Baking Chocolate, grated"

Preheat oven to 350 º. Finely crush 40 of the wafers; place in medium bowl. Add butter and sugar; mix until well blended. Press firmly onto bottom of 9-x 13-inch baking pan. Bake 8 minutes or until lightly browned; set aside.

Pour 1¼ cups of the milk into large bowl. Add dry chocolate pudding mix. Beat with wire whisk 2 minutes or until well blended. Spread over crust; top with remaining 24 wafers.
