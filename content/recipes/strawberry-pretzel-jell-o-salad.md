ingredients:
  - "2 cups crushed pretzels"
  - "3 Tbsp. sugar"
  - "1½ sticks melted butter"
  - "1 (8 oz.) package cream cheese"
  - "1 cup powdered sugar"
  - "1 (12 oz.) container whipped topping"
  - "1 (6 oz.) box strawberry Jell-O®"
  - "2 (10 oz.) packages sliced strawberries"
  - "1½ cups boiling water"
---

Mix crushed pretzels, sugar, and butter together and press into 9-x 13-inch pan. Bake at 400º for 8 minutes and let cool.

Cream together: cream cheese, 1 cup whipped topping, and powdered sugar. Spread mixture on top of pretzel crust. Dissolve Jell-O® in boiling water. Stir in frozen strawberries. When partially set, spread over cream cheese mixture. Refrigerate. When completely set, top with remaining whipped topping.
