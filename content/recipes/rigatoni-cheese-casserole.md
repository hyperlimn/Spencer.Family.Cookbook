ingredients:
  - "½ lb. sweet sausage, browned"
  - "1/3 cup chopped onion"
  - "1 clove garlic, chopped"
  - "6 Tbsp. butter"
  - "2 cups milk"
  - "1½ cups shredded cheddar cheese"
  - "1 cup grated Parmesan cheese"
  - "¼ tsp. salt"
  - "1/8 tsp. pepper"
  - "1 lb. rigatoni"
  - "2-3 fresh, sliced tomatoes"
---

Brown sausage. Cook rigatoni and cool. Mix all ingredients, then add meat & pasta. Place in 9-x 13-inch or larger casserole. Put thick sliced tomatoes on top. Bake for 30 minutes at 350º
