ingredients:
  - "1 pkg. (8 oz.) cream cheese, softened"
  - "1 container (16 oz.) sour cream"
  - "1 can refried beans"
  - "1 packet taco seasoning"
  - "Shredded cheddar or taco cheese"
---

Blend cream cheese, sour cream, refried beans, and taco seasoning in large bowl. Pour into lightly greased baking dish. Sprinkle with cheese and bake at 350 ° until warmed through and cheese is bubbly.

Serve with tortilla chips.
