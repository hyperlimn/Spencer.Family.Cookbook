ingredients:
  - "4 eggs"
  - "1 tsp. vanilla"
  - "2 cups white sugar"
  - "1 cup coconut"
  - "2 sticks melted butter"
  - "1 cup pecans"
  - "½ cup flour"
  - "1 (7 oz.) jar marshmallow crème"
  - "1/3 cup cocoa"
  - "After marshmallow crème melts:"
  - "1 box powdered sugar"
  - "1 stick butter, melted"
  - "1 tsp. vanilla"
  - "6 Tbsp. evaporated milk"
  - "1 cup pecans (for top)"
  - "½ cup cocoa"
---

Beat eggs and sugar until thick. Combine flour, butter, cocoa, vanilla, coconut, and nuts. Mix well. Pour into greased and floured 9-x 13-inch pan. Bake for 30 minutes at 350º. While cake is still hot, spread marshmallow crème over and let stand until it melts.

Icing:

Melt butter and then add all ingredients, except nuts. Spread over cooled cake. Place whole nuts on top, if desired.
