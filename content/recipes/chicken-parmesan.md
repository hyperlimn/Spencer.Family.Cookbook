ingredients:
  - "1 cup Italian flavored bread crumbs"
  - "3 Tbsp. Parmesan cheese"
  - "1 Tbsp. Italian seasoning (optional)"
  - "1 cup Italian salad dressing (your favorite)"
  - "1 lb. skinless, boneless chicken breasts (4 breasts)"
  - "1 small jar of your favorite spaghetti sauce"
  - "2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese, or 4 slices"
---

Preheat oven to 375º. Combine first three ingredients into a bowl. Place Italian dressing into separate bowl. Dip the chicken breast into the Italian dressing and then into the bread crumb mixture. Place onto a greased (I use Pam spray) 9-x 13-inch pan. Bake uncovered for 30 minutes. Remove pan and cover chicken with spaghetti sauce and then cheese. Bake another 15 minutes or until chicken is done. Enjoy! Makes 4 servings.

Variation: Cut chicken into strips before dipping into dressing. Just bake the chicken until done. Dip in BBQ sauce or your favorite dipping sauce.
