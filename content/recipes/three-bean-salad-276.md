ingredients:
  - "1 can green beans"
  - "1 can wax beans"
  - "1 can kidney beans"
  - "1 large onion, cut in rings"
  - "¾ cup vinegar"
  - "2/3 cup sugar"
  - "1/3 cup salad oil"
  - "1 tsp. salt"
---

Drain beans and dissolve sugar in vinegar. Add remaining ingredients and let stand overnight.
