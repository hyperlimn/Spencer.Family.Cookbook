Place 3 chicken breasts in slow cooker with 1 large can Rotel tomatoes until cooked. Remove chicken, shred with fork, and put back in slow cooker. Mix together: 1 heaping Tbsp. minced garlic 1 Tbsp. chili powder 2 Knorr gel packs of chicken stock (or boxed chicken stock) Add to slow cooker and heat through. Add 2 large cans cream of chicken soup and 1 large and 1 small can Rotel. Add milk to desired consistency. Cut up tortillas into strips with pizza cutter and toast in oven. Garnish with these.
