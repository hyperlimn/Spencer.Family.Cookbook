Sprinkle over browned meat 1/3 cup of flour; stir.

Add 1 can of Carnation® evaporated milk mixed with 1 can of water.

Salt and pepper to taste.

Add crushed red pepper for some “zip.” (optional)

Note: May need to add extra milk according to the thickness desired for gravy. Cook over medium heat and let simmer for desired consistency.

Serve over biscuits or toast. Also great for breakfast.
