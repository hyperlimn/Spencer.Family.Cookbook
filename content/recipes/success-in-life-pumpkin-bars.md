ingredients:
  - "4 eggs 2 tsp. baking soda"
  - "2 cups sugar 2 tsp. baking powder"
  - "1 (15 oz.) can pumpkin 1 tsp. salt"
  - "1¼ cups oil 2 tsp. cinnamon"
  - "2 cups flour"
  - "1 stick butter"
  - "1 (8 oz.) pkg. cream cheese"
  - "1 tsp. vanilla"
  - "4 cups powdered sugar (to desired consistency)"
---

Mix together with mixer. Fold in 1 cup coarsely chopped walnuts and 1 cup coconut. Pour into 11-x 17-inch jelly roll pan, greased. Bake at 350º for 35 – 40 minutes. Test with a toothpick. Make sure the center is done, or it will fall. Cool.

Cream Cheese Icing:

Cream butter & cream cheese. Add vanilla & powdered sugar gradually. If desired, add a dash of nutmeg or cloves to the icing.
