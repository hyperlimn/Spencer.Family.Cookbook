Cut core out of cabbage head and cook cabbage in boiling, salted water until leaves remove easily. Cut thick part off end of cabbage leaf with paring knife. Put some meat into middle of each cabbage leaf. Put end over meat, tuck left side of leaf in and then finish rolling and tuck other side into meat.

Use 1 large can tomatoes and 1 large can tomato sauce and pour over cabbage rolls. Cut up pieces of celery and put on top. Place some pieces of Kielbasa around cabbage rolls.

Bake covered at 350º for 1½ hours.
