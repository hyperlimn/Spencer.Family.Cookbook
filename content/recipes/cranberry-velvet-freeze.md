ingredients:
  - "2 cans (16 oz. each) whole berry cranberry sauce"
  - "2 cans (one 20 oz., one 8 oz.) crushed pineapple,"
  - "drained"
  - "1 pkg. (10½ oz.) miniature marshmallows"
  - "1 cup green maraschino cherries, quartered"
  - "1 cup red maraschino cherries, quartered"
  - "1 tsp. lemon juice"
  - "3 cups heavy whipping cream"
---

In a large bowl, combine the cranberry sauce, pineapple, marshmallows, cherries and lemon juice. Fold in whipped cream.

Spoon into an ungreased 9-x 13-inch dish. Cover and freeze overnight. Remove from the freezer 10 minutes before serving.
