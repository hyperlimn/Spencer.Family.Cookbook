ingredients:
  - "1 cup milk"
  - "1 pkg. (3 oz.) cook-and-serve vanilla pudding mix"
  - "1 egg"
  - "½ tsp. ground cinnamon"
  - "8 slices Texas toast"
  - "2 tsp. butter"
---

In a bowl, whisk the milk, pudding mix, egg and cinnamon for 2 minutes or until well blended. Dip toast in pudding mixture, coating both sides.

Melt butter on a griddle; cook French toast for 2 – 4 minutes on each side or until golden brown.
