ingredients:
  - "½ cup sliced almonds"
  - "3 Tbsp. sugar"
  - "2 cups packed torn fresh spinach leaves"
  - "2 cups packed torn Boston lettuce"
  - "2 cups halved strawberries"
  - "1/3 cup Kraft® Special Collection Creamy"
  - "Poppy seed Dressing"
  - "Toss almonds with sugar in medium nonstick"
  - "skillet; cook on medium heat 6 – 8 minutes or"
  - "until sugar forms a golden brown syrup and"
  - "evenly coats the almonds, stirring constantly."
---

Spread into single layer on greased baking sheet or sheet of wax paper; cool. Break into small pieces.

Toss spinach leaves, Boston lettuce, and halved strawberries in a large bowl.

Drizzle with dressing just before serving; mix lightly. Sprinkle with almonds. Serve on chilled plates.
