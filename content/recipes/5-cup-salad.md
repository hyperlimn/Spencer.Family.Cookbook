ingredients:
  - "1 cup sour cream"
  - "1 can pineapple chunks"
  - "1 can mandarin oranges"
  - "1 cup mini marshmallows"
  - "1 cup coconut"
---

Drain pineapple and oranges very well on paper towels. Mix all ingredients together. Make 24 hours ahead or early in the morning.
