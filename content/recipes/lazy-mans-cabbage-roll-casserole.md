ingredients:
  - "1 head cabbage, cut up in pieces, or shredded"
  - "1½ lb. ground beef (or 1 lb. ground beef & ½ lb. sausage)"
  - "1 small onion, chopped"
  - "1 cup cooked rice"
  - "Salt & pepper to taste"
---

Boil cabbage until slightly soft, but done completely; drain water. Brown the ground meat and onion in small amount of olive oil. Add salt & pepper. Cook the rice according to package directions. Mix rice and meat together.

Mix together: 1 can tomato soup with 1 can water 4 oz. can of tomato sauce Add a little sugar, to taste

Layer the cabbage, meat & sauce mixture in a casserole dish, ending with cabbage on top. Bake at 350º for 1 hour, covered. Place shredded cheddar cheese on top and bake uncovered for 30 more minutes.
