ingredients:
  - "2 heads of fresh spinach (washed and broken)"
  - "1 pint fresh strawberries, sliced"
  - "½ red onion, sliced"
  - "½ cup sugar"
  - "¾ tsp. minced onion"
  - "¼ tsp. paprika"
  - "¼ tsp. Worcestershire sauce"
  - "½ cup canola oil"
  - "¼ cup cider vinegar"
  - "2 Tbsp. sesame or poppy seeds"
  - "Toss together spinach, strawberries, and onion."
---

Combine all ingredients for the dressing in a blender except for the sesame or poppy seeds. Once ingredients are blended, stir seeds into mixture. Just prior to serving, add dressing to main body of salad and toss.

Refreshing and delicious!
