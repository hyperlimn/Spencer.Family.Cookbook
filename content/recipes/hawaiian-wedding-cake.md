ingredients:
  - "2 cups flour"
  - "1 cup sugar"
  - "2 eggs"
  - "1 tsp. baking soda"
  - "1 can crushed pineapple, undrained"
  - "1 cup coconut"
  - "1 cup walnuts, chopped"
  - "1 cup powdered sugar"
  - "1 (8 oz.) cream cheese, softened"
  - "1 (8 oz.) container whipped topping"
  - "1 stick butter"
---

Mix all ingredients together by hand. Pour into ungreased 9-x 13-inch pan. Bake at 350º for 25-30 minutes. Cool and frost. Keep cake in refrigerator.

Topping:

Beat all ingredients together. Frost cake.
