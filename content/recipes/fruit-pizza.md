ingredients:
  - "1 box Duncan Hines Sugar Cookie Mix"
  - "1 (8 oz.) pkg. cream cheese, softened"
  - "1 cup confectioners’ sugar"
  - "1 large can crushed pineapple, well drained"
  - "1 large can mandarin oranges, well drained"
  - "3 – 4 bananas, sliced (dip in lemon juice or fruit fresh)"
  - "1 quart strawberries, halved"
  - "1 pkg. strawberry glaze (or recipe below)"
  - "Prepare cookie mix according to package directions for"
  - "“rolled and cut cookies.” Pat into large pizza pan."
---

Bake 9 – 10 minutes at 350º or until lightly browned. Mix cream cheese and confectioners’ sugar until fluffy and spread on cooled crust. Layer pineapple, bananas, oranges; then place strawberries over cream cheese. Spoon glaze over fruit. Refrigerate. Glaze: 2 cups sugar 2 Tbsp. light Karo® syrup 6 Tbsp. cornstarch 1 small pkg. strawberry Jell-O® 2 cups water

Combine all ingredients (except Jell-O® and cook until thick and clear, stirring constantly. Stir in Jell-O®. Cool in refrigerator before putting on pizza.
