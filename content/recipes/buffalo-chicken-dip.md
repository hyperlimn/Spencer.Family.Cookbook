ingredients:
  - "2 ½ lbs. Shredded chicken (or 2 extra large"
  - "Costco cans chunk chicken, drained)"
  - "2 (8 oz.) pkgs. Cream cheese, softened"
  - "1 cup Ranch dressing"
  - "1 cup Frank’s red hot sauce"
  - "8 oz. Colby Jack shredded or Mexican Blend"
  - "shredded cheese"
---

Heat chicken and hot sauce in skillet over medium heat. Do not use non-stick skillet. Shred chicken with fork.

Stir in cream cheese and melt. Stir in Ranch dressing and cheese. Pour into ungreased glass lasagna dish. Bake at 350º until hot and bubbly and golden brown. May also use a slow cooker. Serve with Scoops, tortilla chips, crackers, or celery sticks.
