  - "1 can (10¾ oz.) condensed cream of onion soup,"
  - "undiluted"
  - "5 oz. Velveeta cheese, cubed"
  - "1/3 cup mayonnaise"
  - "4 cups cubed cooked turkey"
  - "1 pkg. (16 oz.) frozen broccoli cuts, thawed"
  - "1½ cups cooked white rice"
  - "1½ cups cooked wild rice"
  - "1 can (8 oz.) sliced water chestnuts, drained (optional)"
  - "1 jar (4 oz.) sliced mushrooms, drained"
  - "1½ to 2 cups salad croutons"
---

In a large bowl, combine the soups, cheese, and mayonnaise. Stir in the turkey, broccoli, rice, water chestnuts, and mushrooms. Transfer to a greased 9-x 13-inch baking dish. Bake, uncovered, at 350 for 30 minutes; stir. Sprinkle with croutons. Bake 8 – 12 minutes longer or until bubbly. Note: Reduced-fat or fat-free mayonnaise is NOT recommended for this recipe.
