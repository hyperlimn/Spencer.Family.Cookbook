  - "Simmer:"
  - "2 ½ sticks butter"
  - "5 – 6 onions, thinly sliced"
  - "Salt & pepper to taste"
  - "1 pkg. wide noodles, cook as directed"
---

Mix onions and noodles.

French’s® instant mashed potatoes, use 2 pkgs. in box. (Fresh potatoes do not work.)

Put half noodle mixture on bottom of 9-x 13-inch pan. Spread all mashed potatoes on noodles. Add layer of remaining noodles. Grate cheddar or Colby cheese on top. Cover with foil. Bake for ½ hour at 350º.
