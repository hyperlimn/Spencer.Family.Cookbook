ingredients:
  - "½ cup shortening"
  - "1 cup sugar"
  - "3 small bananas (really ripened)"
  - "2 eggs, beaten"
  - "8 Tbsp. sour cream (½ cup)"
  - "1 tsp. soda"
  - "1 tsp. baking powder"
  - "2 ½ cups all-purpose flour"
  - "1 cup chopped walnuts"
---

Cream shortening and sugar together. Add eggs. Mash bananas and add to mixture. Add sour cream. Mix together flour, baking soda, baking powder, and add to mixture. Add nuts. Pour mixture into 2 loaf pans. Bake at 350º for 40 minutes .
