Pour 2 cans pie filling of your choice in 9-x 13-inch baking dish.

Drop lumps of sugar cookie dough onto filling.

Sprinkle with mixture of cinnamon and sugar.

Bake at 350º for 30 – 40 minutes or until golden brown.
