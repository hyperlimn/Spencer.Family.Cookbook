ingredients:
  - "2 cups cubed cooked chicken"
  - "1 cup chopped celery"
  - "1 cup mayonnaise"
  - "½-1 tsp. curry powder"
  - "1 can (20 oz.) chunk pineapple, drained"
  - "2 large firm bananas, sliced"
  - "1 can (11 oz.) mandarin oranges, drained"
  - "½ cup flaked coconut"
  - "Salad greens, optional"
  - "¾ cup salted peanuts or cashew halves"
---

Place chicken and celery in large bowl. Combine mayonnaise and curry powder; add to chicken mixture and mix well. Cover and chill for at least 30 minutes. Before serving, add the pineapple, bananas, oranges, and coconut; toss gently. Serve on salad greens if desired. Sprinkle with nuts.
