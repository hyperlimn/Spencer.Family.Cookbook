ingredients:
  - "2 lbs. ground beef"
  - "1 cup bread crumbs"
  - "4 eggs"
  - "2 Tbsp. parsley flakes"
  - "2 tsp. salt"
  - "¼ tsp. pepper"
  - "1 crushed garlic clove"
  - "3 Tbsp. parmesan cheese"
---

Mix all ingredients together. Work with hands by dipping hands into cool water before rolling. This gives meatballs a smooth surface. Put in spaghetti sauce and cook for at least 1 hour.
