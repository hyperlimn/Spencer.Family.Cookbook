ingredients:
  - "2 lbs. ground beef"
  - "2 onions, diced"
  - "½ cup celery, diced"
  - "2 tsp. lemon juice"
  - "2 tsp. brown sugar"
  - "2 tsp. dry mustard"
  - "1 cup tomato sauce"
  - "1 cup ketchup"
  - "1 Tbsp. chili powder"
  - "½ cup water"
---

Brown meat with onions & celery. Add remaining ingredients. Simmer for 1 – 1½ hours.
