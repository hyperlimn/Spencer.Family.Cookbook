ingredients:
  - "8 bacon strips"
  - "4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts"
  - "¼ tsp. pepper"
  - "1 (10¾ oz.) can condensed cream of onion or cream"
  - "of chicken soup, undiluted"
  - "¼ cup chicken broth"
  - "¼ cup sliced almonds, toasted"
---

In a microwave, cook bacon on paper towels on high for 1 – 3 minutes or until partially cooked. Wrap 2 bacon strips around each chicken breast. Sprinkle with pepper. Arrange in an 8-inch square, microwave safe dish. Cover and microwave on high for 7 minutes. Drain.

In a bowl, combine soup & broth. Cover and microwave for 2 minutes. Spoon around chicken. Cook, uncovered 5 – 7 minutes longer or until juices run clear. Let stand 5 minutes before serving. Sprinkle with almonds.
