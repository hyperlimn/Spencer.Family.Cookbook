Drain 1 cup pear juice and heat. Use this to dissolve Jell-O®. Do not add cold water. Mash pears and add to gelatin. Add softened cream cheese. Beat with an electric mixer. Fold in whipping cream and cut up cherries. Mix together. Pour mixture into a mold or 9-x 13-inch glass dish and refrigerate until set.

(Makes a nice Christmas salad.)

Frosted 7-Up® Fruit

Jell-O® Salad Kathy Spencer

2 (3 oz.) pkgs. orange Jell-O® ( I use sugar free) 2 cups boiling water 2 cups 7-Up® ( I use diet soda) 1 can crushed, drained pineapple in its own juice 2 lg. bananas, sliced 1 cup miniature marshmallows

Topping: ½ cup sugar 1 tbsp. flour 1 cup pineapple juice 1 egg, beaten 2 tbsp. butter 1 cup whipped cream or whipped topping ¼ cup finely shredded cheese (optional)

Dissolve gelatin in boiling water. Add soda and chill until partially set. Add remaining ingredients and chill. Create a topping by combining the sugar and flour in a saucepan. Stir in pineapple juice and egg. Continue stirring constantly until mixture is thick; add butter. Cool. Fold in whipped topping and spread over gelatin. If desired, sprinkle with cheese. (My personal favorite.)
