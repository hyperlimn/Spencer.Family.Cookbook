ingredients:
  - "1 lg. can Bartlett pears"
  - "1 pkg. lime Jell-O® (3 oz.)"
  - "2 lg. pkgs. cream cheese, softened"
  - "½ pt. whipping cream, whipped"
  - "1 small bottle maraschino cherries"
---

Drain 1 cup pear juice and heat. Use this to dissolve Jell-O®. Do not add cold water. Mash pears and add to gelatin. Add softened cream cheese. Beat with an electric mixer. Fold in whipping cream and cut up cherries. Mix together. Pour mixture into a mold or 9-x 13-inch glass dish and refrigerate until set.

(Makes a nice Christmas salad.)
