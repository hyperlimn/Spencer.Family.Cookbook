ingredients:
  - "1 cup peanut butter"
  - "1½ cups graham cracker crumbs"
  - "1 cup confectioners’ sugar"
  - "1 cup butter, melted"
---

Mix ingredients together and press into lightly buttered 9 x 13-inch pan. Refrigerate for 10 minutes.

Melt a 12 oz. package of chocolate chips over hot water. Pour over peanut butter mixture. Refrigerate for 30 minutes. (May need extra crumbs & confectioners’ sugar)
