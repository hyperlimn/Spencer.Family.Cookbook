Beat well with mixer. Pour into well greased and floured 9 x 13-inch pan. Bake for 40 minutes at 350º.

Icing:

Boil sugar and water until it forms a soft ball when dropped in cold water. Beat egg whites with pinch of cream of tartar added. Add 1 tsp. vanilla and Karo® syrup to egg whites. Beat until stiff.
