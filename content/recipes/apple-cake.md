ingredients:
  - "2½ cups flour 1 tsp. cinnamon"
  - "1 tsp. baking powder 2 cups sugar"
  - "1 tsp. baking soda 1 cup vegetable oil"
  - "1 tsp. salt 2 eggs"
  - "1 tsp. vanilla 1 cup pecans"
  - "3 cups chopped apples 1 cup butterscotch chips"
  - "(add more )"
---

Mix first 5 ingredients together and set aside. Mix (by hand) sugar, oil, eggs, and vanilla until blended. Add flour mixture until blended. (It will have the consistency of cookie dough.) Stir in apples and pecans. Spread in greased 9-x 13-inch pan. Sprinkle butterscotch chips on top. Bake at 350º for 1 hour.
