ingredients:
  - "1 (3 oz.) pkg. cream cheese, softened"
  - "2 Tbsp. butter"
  - "2 Tbsp. mayonnaise"
  - "6 oz. pineapple, peach, or apricot preserves"
  - "1 Tbsp. freshly squeezed lemon juice"
  - "¼ tsp. curry powder"
  - "½ cup heavy whipping cream, whipped"
---

Blend cream cheese & mayonnaise by hand until smooth. Stir in preserves, lemon juice, & curry powder. Chill until about 1 hour before serving. Stir in whipped cream & refrigerate until serving time.

**Unusually delicious dressing for all kinds of fruit salads.
