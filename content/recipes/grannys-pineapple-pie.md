Stir in 2 cans crushed pineapple. Cook over medium heat, stirring until it thickens. Pour into pastry-lined pie pan and top with pie crust. (I use Pillsbury® ready-made pie crust.) Bake at 425º for 25-30 min.

Liz’s favorite!
