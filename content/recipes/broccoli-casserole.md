ingredients:
  - "2 pkgs. frozen chopped broccoli"
  - "2 cans cream of mushroom soup"
  - "1 can Carnation® evaporated milk"
  - "1 tsp. salt"
  - "1½-2 sticks of butter"
  - "½ cup chopped onion (optional)"
  - "1 box or 2 cups Minute Rice (cooked)"
  - "1/2 of large block of Velveeta cheese"
  - "In large skillet, sauté onions with butter until"
  - "tender."
---

Add frozen broccoli.

Add soup, canned milk, and salt; cook until thawed.

Add cubed Velveeta cheese.

Put cooked rice in bowl, pour cooked broccoli mix over rice, and mix all together Bake in large casserole, covered, at 350º for 1 hour.
