ingredients:
  - "1 (2 layer) size white cake mix"
  - "Small box instant vanilla pudding"
  - "4 eggs"
  - "1 cup water"
  - "¼ cup oil"
  - "3 oz. package raspberry Jell-O®"
  - "1 cup boiling water"
  - "1 cup cold water"
---

Combine cake mix, pudding mix, oil, eggs, 1 cup water. Blend well. Beat with electric mixer at medium speed for 4 minutes. Pour into greased, floured 9-x 13-inch pan. Bake at 350º for 45 – 50 minutes. Cool for 15 minutes.

Dissolve gelatin in boiling water and add cold water. Poke holes in cake with wooden spoon handle. Pour gelatin over cake. Chill for 3 – 4 hours. Top with Cool Whip® when serving.
