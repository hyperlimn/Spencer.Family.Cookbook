ingredients:
  - "4 carrots, cut in small pieces"
  - "4 potatoes"
  - "2 lbs. beef stew meat"
  - "1 – 1½ cups water"
  - "1 beef bouillon cube"
  - "1 tsp. Worcestershire sauce"
  - "Dash garlic powder"
  - "½ tsp. pepper"
  - "1 tsp. Paprika"
  - "2 onions, quartered"
  - "2 stalks celery, cut in chunks"
---

Place in slow cooker in order listed above, beginning with carrots on bottom. Cook on low for 10 – 12 hours or high for 4 – 6 hours.
