ingredients:
  - "1 pkg. (8 oz.) cream cheese, softened"
  - "1 can (18 oz.) pumpkin pie mix"
  - "2 cups powdered sugar"
  - "½ to 1 tsp. ground cinnamon"
  - "¼ to ½ tsp. ground ginger"
---

Beat cream cheese in a mixing bowl until smooth. Add pumpkin pie mix; beat well. Add sugar, cinnamon and ginger; beat until smooth. Serve with gingersnaps.
