ingredients:
  - "1 cup cheddar cheese, shredded"
  - "½ cup mozzarella cheese, shredded"
  - "¼ cup Parmesan cheese"
  - "4-5 green onions, finely chopped"
  - "Mayonnaise"
  - "Party rye bread"
---

Mix all ingredients together, using enough mayon- naise to make cheese spreadable. Line cookie sheet with slices of party rye bread. Heap cheese mixture onto bread slices. Put under broiler or bake at 350º until cheese is bubbly — bread should be crisp. Enjoy while hot!

Note: I usually bake until toast is crisp and then broil to melt cheese, if needed.
