ingredients:
  - "2 lbs. frozen hash browns"
  - "½ cup melted butter"
  - "1 can cream of chicken soup"
  - "1 pint sour cream"
  - "½ cup chopped onion"
  - "2 cups grated cheddar cheese"
  - "1 tsp. salt"
  - "¼ tsp. pepper"
  - "2 cups crushed cornflakes"
  - "¼ cup melted butter"
---

Defrost hash browns. Combine next 7 ingredients and mix with hash browns. Put in a 3-quart casserole. Sauté corn flakes in butter and sprinkle on top. Cover and bake at 350º for about 40 minutes.
