Mix # 1: 1 cup brown sugar, packed ¼ cup cocoa Set aside.

Mix # 2: 1 cup flour 2 Tbsp. oil 2 tsp. baking powder ¾ cup sugar 2 Tbsp. cocoa ¼ tsp. salt ½ cup milk 1 cup nuts

Stir ingredients together. Spread in 9-inch square pan (ungreased). Sift or sprinkle Mixture number 1 on top of Mixture number 2. Pour 1¾ cups hot water on top of mixture in pan. Bake for 45 minutes at 350º. When cutting, invert before placing on dish. Top with whipped cream or ice cream.
