Stir until boiling. Boil gently for 2 minutes. Without stirring, pour over the base.

Sprinkle over filling: 2½ cups chopped nuts

Bake at 375º for 15-20 more minutes or until bubbly. Sprinkle with 1 cup chocolate chips as soon as removed from oven. Knife edge of pan immediately.
