ingredients:
  - "1 cup white sugar"
  - "½ cup white Karo® syrup"
  - "¼ cup melted butter"
  - "3 eggs, well beaten"
  - "1 cup pecans (small pieces)"
  - "1 unbaked Pillsbury® pie shell (9-inch)"
---

Combine sugar, syrup, and melted butter. Add beaten eggs and pecans to syrup mixture, mixing thoroughly. Pour filling into pie crust. Bake at 375º for approximately 35 minutes until golden brown, about 40 – 45 minutes.
