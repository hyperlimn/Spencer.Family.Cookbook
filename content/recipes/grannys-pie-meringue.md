Cook until thick (cool completely).

Beat 6 egg whites until stiff, and add 12 Tbsp. white sugar.

Then add cooled ingredients to egg whites and mix well until fluffy.

Spread on top of pie.

Brown meringue under broiler until peaks look golden.

Makes enough meringue for 2 pies.
