ingredients:
  - "2 lbs. Round steak, 1-inch thick"
  - "Flour"
  - "1 large onion"
  - "1 green pepper"
  - "4 stalks celery"
  - "3 large carrots"
  - "1 (28 oz.) can tomatoes"
  - "1/3 cup ketchup"
  - "1 (8 oz.) can tomato sauce"
  - "2 tsp. Worcestershire sauce"
  - "Salt & pepper to taste"
  - "1 tsp. Sugar"
---

Roll meat in flour and brown in olive oil. Place in large, deep baking dish. Cut onion, pepper, celery, and carrots in large pieces and add to the meat. Mix remaining ingredients and pour over the meat. Bake, covered, for 2 – 2½ hours at 325º or until tender.
