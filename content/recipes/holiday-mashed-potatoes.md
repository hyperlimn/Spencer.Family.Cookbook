Beat until smooth.

Mix the following & add to potatoes:

2 eggs lightly beaten 1 tsp. salt Dash of pepper

Grease 9-x13-inch pan. Pour potatoes into pan, cover, and refrigerate overnight. Bake at 350º for 45 minutes until light brown on top.
