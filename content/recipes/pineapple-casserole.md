ingredients:
  - "1 can pineapple chunks (20 oz. can),"
  - "* reserve 3 Tbsp. juice"
  - "3 Tbsp. flour"
  - "½ cup sugar"
  - "1 cup shredded cheddar cheese"
  - "1 stick butter (melted)"
  - "30 Ritz® crackers (1 sleeve), crumbled"
---

Drain pineapple, reserving 3 Tbsp. juice. Combine flour, sugar, and juice; mix well. Add to pineapple and cheese. Mix together and spread into greased 1-qt. casserole dish. Combine butter and cracker crumbs. Spread over pineapple mixture. Bake at 350º for 20-30 minutes or until lightly browned. May be served hot or cold. Double recipe for 9-x 13-inch dish.
