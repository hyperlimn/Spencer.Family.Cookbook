5 lbs. Idaho potatoes, peeled & sliced Butter Carnation® evaporated milk All-purpose flour

Grease dish. Place one layer of potatoes in dish. Sprinkle with salt & pepper, flour, and pats of butter. Continue to layer until all potatoes are gone. Pour canned milk over potatoes, completely covering. If you need more milk, you may use regular milk. Cover and place in 425º oven. Bake for 1 to 1 1/2 hours, or until potatoes are tender. Sprinkle with cheese for the last few minutes of cooking. (You will have to learn the consistency of the flour, etc. Practice makes perfect!)
