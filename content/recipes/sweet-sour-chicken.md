ingredients:
  - "4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts"
  - "½ cup flour with salt & pepper"
  - "1/3 cup oil or shortening"
  - "Shake chicken in flour; brown both sides well in"
  - "hot oil. Put in 9-x 13-inch baking dish. Bake at 350º"
  - "for 20 minutes."
  - "Sauce:"
  - "1 (13 oz.) can chopped pineapple"
  - "1¼ cups juice from pineapple (add water if needed)"
  - "2/3 cup vinegar"
  - "¾ cup sugar"
  - "¼ tsp. ginger"
  - "1 chicken bouillon cube"
  - "2 Tbsp. cornstarch"
  - "1 Tbsp. soy sauce"
  - "1 (10 oz.) can mandarin oranges"
  - "½ cup maraschino cherries"
  - "Mix all ingredients except pineapple, oranges, and"
  - "cherries. Boil for 2 minutes, stirring. Pour sauce over"
  - "chicken. Add pineapple, oranges, and cherries."
  - "Baste and bake 20 minutes more. Accompany with rice."
