1 white cake mix 1 can or bottle cream of coconut 1 can sweetened condensed milk 8 oz. container frozen whipped topping, thawed 6-8 oz. flaked coconut

Mix cake mix according to directions. When cake is done, take out of oven. While still hot, poke holes in cake with straw or wooden spoon handle. Mix cream of coconut and sweet milk together; then pour over cake. After cake cools, spread whipped topping on top and sprinkle with flaked coconut . Refrigerate.
