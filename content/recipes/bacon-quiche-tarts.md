ingredients:
  - "2 pkgs. (3 oz. each) cream cheese, softened"
  - "5 tsp. milk"
  - "2 eggs"
  - "½ cup shredded Colby cheese"
  - "2 Tbsp. chopped green pepper"
  - "1 Tbsp. finely chopped onion"
  - "1 tube (8 oz.) refrigerated crescent rolls"
  - "5 bacon strips, cooked and crumbled"
---

In a small mixing bowl, beat cream cheese and In a separate skillet, scramble the eggs. Add to milk until smooth. Add the eggs, cheese, green pepper, and onion; mix well.

Separate dough into 8 triangles; press onto the bottom and up the sides of greased muffin cups. Sprinkle half of the bacon into cups. Pour egg mixture over bacon; top with remaining bacon. Bake, uncovered, at 375º for 18-22 minutes or until a knife comes out clean. Serve warm.
