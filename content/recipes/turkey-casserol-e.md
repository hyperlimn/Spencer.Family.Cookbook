  - "Cooked turkey, chopped"
  - "StoveTop® stuffing mix"

Mix soup and sour cream together. Spread into 9-x 13-inch pan. Add layer of cooked & chopped turkey. Top with 1 box stuffing mix, prepared according to package directions.

Bake uncovered for 1 hour at 350º
