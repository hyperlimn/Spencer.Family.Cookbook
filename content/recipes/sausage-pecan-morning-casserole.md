ingredients:
  - "1 loaf (16 oz.) raisin bread"
  - "10 Jones Golden Brown sausage links"
  - "(or your favorite ground sausage)"
  - "6 eggs ¼ tsp. ground nutmeg"
  - "1 ½ cups milk 1 cup packed brown sugar"
  - "1 ½ cups half and half ½ cup butter, softened"
  - "1 tsp. vanilla extract 2 Tbsp. maple syrup"
  - "¼ tsp. ground cinnamon 1 cup coarsely chopped pecans"
---

1. Prepare a 9-x 13-inch glass baking pan with nonstick spray. Cube raisin bread and put in the baking dish. 2. Brown sausage; drain and cut into bite-sized pieces. Mix with bread in the baking dish. 3. In a large mixing bowl, beat eggs, milk, half and half, vanilla, nutmeg and cinnamon until blended. Pour over bread and sausage. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for 8 hours or overnight. 4. Heat oven to 350º. Combine brown sugar, butter, syrup, and pecans. Spoon by teaspoonfuls over casserole. Bake uncovered for 35 to 40 minutes, or until knife inserted comes out clean.
