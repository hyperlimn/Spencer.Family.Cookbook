ingredients:
  - "2 Tbsp. butter"
  - "2 Tbsp. flour"
  - "1 tsp. salt"
  - "½ pint sour cream"
  - "½ lb. sharp cheddar cheese"
  - "¼ tsp. pepper"
  - "1 tsp. sugar"
  - "½ Tbsp. grated onion"
  - "2 cups Corn Flakes®"
  - "2 Tbsp. melted butter"
  - "2 pkgs. frozen French cut green beans"
---

Melt butter and stir in flour, salt, pepper, sugar, and onions. Add sour cream and stir until thick. Fold in cooked, drained green beans. Grate onions and sprinkle over beans. Place in 9-x 13-inch casserole. Crush Corn Flakes® and mix with butter. Put on top of casserole. Bake at 350º for 45 minutes or until golden brown and bubbly.
