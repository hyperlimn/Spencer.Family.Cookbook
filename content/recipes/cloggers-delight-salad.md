ingredients:
  - "1 package Ramen® noodle soup"
  - "8 oz. package coleslaw"
  - "3 – 4 oz. package slivered almonds"
  - "¼ cup toasted sunflower seeds"
  - "2 – 3 chopped green onions"
  - "Dressing:"
  - "½ cup canola oil"
  - "1/3 cup cider vinegar"
  - "¼ cup sugar"
  - "Seasoning packet from Ramen® noodles"
  - "Break up Ramen® noodles in large bowl (do not"
  - "cook). Combine noodles, coleslaw, nuts, & onions."
---

Mix dressing & pour over salad. Toss to coat. Add cooked, cubed chicken, if desired.
