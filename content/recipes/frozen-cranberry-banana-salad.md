ingredients:
  - "1 can (20 oz.) pineapple tidbits"
  - "5 medium firm bananas, halved lengthwise and"
  - "sliced"
  - "1 can (16 oz.) whole-berry cranberry sauce"
  - "½ cup sugar"
  - "1 carton (12 oz.) frozen whipped topping, thawed"
  - "½ cup chopped walnuts"
---

Drain pineapple juice into a medium bowl; set pineapple aside. Add bananas to the juice. In a large bowl, combine cranberry sauce and sugar. Remove bananas, discarding juice, and add to cranberry mixture. Stir in pineapple, whipped topping and nuts. Pour into a 9-x 13-inch dish. Freeze until solid. Remove from the freezer 15 minutes before cutting.
