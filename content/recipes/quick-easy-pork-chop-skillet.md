ingredients:
  - "4 bone-in pork chops, ¾ inch thick"
  - "1 tsp. oil"
  - "¼ cup Kraft® Light Balsamic Vinaigrette"
  - "Reduced Fat Dressing, divided"
  - "1 small onion, sliced"
  - "1 clove garlic, minced"
  - "1 tsp. dried rosemary leaves"
  - "1 can (15¼ oz.) pear halves in juice, undrained"
---

Brown chops in oil in nonstick skillet on medium high heat for 3minutes on each side. Remove from skillet; set aside.

Add 2 Tbsp. of the dressing, onions, garlic, and rosemary to skillet. Cook 3 minutes.

Return chops to skillet. Add pears and remaining 2 Tbsp. dressing. Simmer on medium heat 10 minutes or until chops are cooked through. Arrange chops on platter; spoon sauce over chops.
