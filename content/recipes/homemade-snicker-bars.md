1 layer: Melt 1 cup chocolate chips, ¼ cup peanut butter, and ¼ cup butterscotch chips. Spread in the bottom of a greased 9-x 13-inch cake pan. Let cool.

2 layer: Boil 1 cup sugar, ¼ cup canned milk, and ¼ cup butter for 5 minutes. Add ¼ cup peanut butter, 1½ cups marshmallow crème, and 1 tsp. vanilla. Mix in 1¼ cup salted peanuts. Spread over first layer and cool.

3 layer: Melt one package (11.5 oz.) caramels and 4 Tbsp. Carnation® milk . Spread over second layer and cool.

4 layer: Repeat First Layer. Spread over caramels and cool. Cut into squares. Keep refrigerated.
