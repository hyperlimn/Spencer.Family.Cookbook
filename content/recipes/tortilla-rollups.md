ingredients:
  - "1 pkg. flour tortillas"
  - "2 (8 oz.) pkgs. cream cheese, softened"
  - "1 pkg. Hidden Valley® Ranch dressing"
  - "1 pkg. shredded cheddar cheese"
---

Mix softened cream cheese with dry Ranch dressing. Spread on tortillas. Sprinkle with cheese. Roll up and chill. Cut in slices and serve with thick & chunky salsa.
