1 (15 or 18 oz.) pkg. chocolate chip cookies* 1 cup milk 1 (9 oz.) commercial graham cracker crust 1 (8 oz.) container frozen whipped topping, thawed

Dip 8 cookies in milk, and place in a single layer in crust. Top with 1/3 of whipped topping. Dip 8 more cookies in milk, and place on top; spread with 1/3 of cool whip. Repeat layers with 8 more cookies, milk, and remaining whipped topping. Crumble 2 chocolate chip cookies and sprinkle over pie. Cover and chill 8 hours before serving.

*You can substitute your favorite cookie for the chocolate chip. For instance, Oreo cookies are good with an Oreo crust.
