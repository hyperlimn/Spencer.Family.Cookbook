ingredients:
  - "4 pork chops, about ¾-inch thick"
  - "1 onion, sliced"
  - "2 cups sliced mushrooms (optional)"
  - "2 Tbsp. balsamic vinaigrette dressing"
  - "1½ cups Minute White Rice, uncooked"
  - "1 can (10½ oz.) condensed chicken broth"
  - "1 broth can water"
  - "2 red apples, sliced"
  - "1 tsp. dried thyme leaves"
  - "Spray large nonstick skillet with cooking spray."
---

Add chops; cook on medium-high heat for 4 minutes on each side or until browned.

Add onion, mushrooms, and dressing; cook an additional 3 minutes.

Stir in rice, broth, water, apples, and thyme. Reduce heat to medium; simmer for 10 minutes or until heated through.
