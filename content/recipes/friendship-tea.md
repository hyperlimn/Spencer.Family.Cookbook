ingredients:
  - "1 (10 – 12 oz.) Tang®"
  - "1 pkg. unsweetened lemonade (Kool-Aid®)"
  - "1 cup instant iced tea"
  - "½ cup sugar"
  - "1 tsp. cinnamon"
---

Mix all ingredients. Shake well to blend. Keep in glass jar. Use 2 – 3 Tbsp. of dry mixture added to boiling water to serve.
