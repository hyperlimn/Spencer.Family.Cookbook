ingredients:
  - "Custard sauce:"
  - "4 cups milk"
  - "4 eggs"
  - "¾ cup sugar"
  - "1 Tbsp. flour"
  - "2 tsp. vanilla"
---

Heat milk until scalding in saucepan. Whisk; beat eggs with sugar and flour. Pour small amount of hot milk into egg mixture, stirring well. Add egg mixture to remaining hot milk.

Cook mixture over medium heat, stirring constantly until coats metal spoon. Remove from heat and add vanilla.
