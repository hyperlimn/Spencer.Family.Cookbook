Mix together:

¾ cup sugar 2 Tbsp. cornstarch

Add 2 eggs ¼ cup reserved pineapple juice 1 tsp. Vanilla

Mix well together. Place into casserole dish and sprinkle with cinnamon. Bake uncovered for 45 minutes-1 hour at 350º.
