ingredients:
  - "1 cup all-purpose flour"
  - "1 cup quick-cooking oats"
  - "1 cup packed brown sugar"
  - "½ tsp. salt"
  - "½ cup shortening"
  - "* 2 ½ cups fresh or frozen blueberries"
  - "¼ cup sugar"
---

In a large bowl, combine the flour, oats, brown sugar, and salt. Cut in shortening until crumbly. Press half of the mixture into a greased 9-inch square baking dish. Bake at 350º for 10 minutes or until brown around the edges.

Combine the blueberries and sugar; sprinkle over crust. Top with remaining oat mixture; press down gently. Bake 35 – 40 minutes longer or until golden brown. Serve warm.

*Note: If using frozen blueberries, do not thaw.
