Cut into 3 pieces while partially frozen. Do not let bread rise.

Place on buttered cookie sheet.

Spread surface of counter or table with oil. Place dough ball (1 of the 3 pieces) on table and spread out with palms of hands. Place 4 rows of small pepperoni slices (about 15 slices) on dough, overlapping pepperoni. Sprinkle with shredded mozzarella cheese. Bake at 350º-400º for 10 minutes.

Delicious!
