Heat on stove top until boiling. Place 1 (24 oz.) pkg. Reames® Frozen Noodles into broth Salt and Pepper to taste. Cook according to directions on package.

***To make noodles more appealing, you can add a drop or 2 of yellow food coloring. You may also add canned white chicken breast, drained or breast of chicken that has been boiled and cut into small pieces.
