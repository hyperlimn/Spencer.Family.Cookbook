ingredients:
  - "5 potatoes"
  - "½ stick butter, softened"
  - "2 cups sour cream"
  - "2 cups cheddar cheese"
  - "½ cup chopped onion"
  - "Salt & pepper to taste"
---

Cook potatoes in skins until done, but firm. Drain and refrigerate overnight. Grate potatoes. Sprinkle with cheese. Mix together sour cream, butter, onion, salt, and pepper. Gently fold in above mixture. Bake at 375º for 30 – 45 minutes.
