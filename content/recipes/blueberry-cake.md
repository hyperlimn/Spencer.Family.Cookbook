ingredients:
  - "1 box Duncan Hines® cake mix (Make a"
  - "2-layer cake according to directions on box.)"
  - "1 (8 oz.) pkg. cream cheese"
  - "1 can blueberry pie filling"
  - "1 lg. container frozen whipped topping, thawed"
  - "1 box confectioners’ sugar"
---

Cream whipped topping, cream cheese, and confectioners’ sugar together with a mixer. Place in refrigerator to cool. After baking cake according to directions on box, let cool completely. Frost first layer with about ½ inch of frosting. Add second layer and frost, making a nest-like center in the frosting. Pour blueberry filling in the middle of the nest-like center and let it drizzle down the sides of the cake.

Refrigerate.

Delicious and refreshing!
