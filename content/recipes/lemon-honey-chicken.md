ingredients:
  - "1 (14½ oz.) can chicken broth"
  - "2 medium carrots, shredded"
  - "4 cups Pepperidge Farm® Herb Seasoned Stuffing"
  - "6 boneless, skinless chicken breasts"
  - "2 Tbsp. each honey and lemon juice"
  - "1 Tbsp. chopped fresh parsley"
  - "3 lemon slices cut in half"
---

In saucepan, mix broth and carrots. Over medium-high heat, heat to a boil; remove from heat. Add stuffing. Mix lightly.

Spoon into greased 3-quart, shallow baking dish. Arrange chicken over stuffing. Bake at 375º for 50 minutes.

Mix honey, lemon juice, and parsley. Brush over chicken. Place lemon slices on chicken. Bake 10 minutes or until chicken is done and no longer pink. Stir stuffing.
