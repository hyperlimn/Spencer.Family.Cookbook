Mix together:

1 ¼ cup white sugar 1 medium onion, chopped finely 2 Tbsp. vinegar 2 Tbsp. prepared horseradish

Pour over pickles and shake often over a few days. Better if prepared 3-4 days ahead of time.

This mixture may be used over and over for more pickles by adding 1½ tsp. vinegar, ¾ cup sugar, 1 Tbsp. horseradish, celery seed, and onion.

Shake jar periodically while stored in refrigerator.
