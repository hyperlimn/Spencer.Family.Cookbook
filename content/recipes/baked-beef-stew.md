ingredients:
  - "2 lbs. lean beef stew meat, cut in 1-inch cubes"
  - "1 (14½ oz.) can diced tomatoes, undrained"
  - "1 cup water"
  - "3 Tbsp. quick cooking tapioca"
  - "2 tsp. sugar"
  - "1½ tsp. salt"
  - "½ tsp. pepper"
  - "2 lbs. lean beef stew meat, cut into 1-inch cubes"
  - "3 medium potatoes, peeled & quartered"
  - "2 celery ribs, cut into ¾-inch chunks"
  - "1 medium onion, cut into chunks"
  - "1 slice bread, cubed"
---

In a large bowl, mix together first 6 ingredients. Mix well. Add remaining ingredients and mix well. Pour into greased 9-x 13-inch or 3-quart baking dish. Cover and bake at 375º for 2 hours or until meat & vegetables are tender. Serve in bowls. This can also be made in a slow cooker.
