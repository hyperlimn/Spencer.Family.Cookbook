ingredients:
  - "2 cans (10 3/4 oz. each) Campbell’s® Condensed"
  - "Golden Mushroom Soup"
  - "½ cup water"
  - "1 Tbsp. packed brown sugar"
  - "1 Tbsp. Worcestershire sauce"
  - "1 tsp. Dried thyme leaves, crushed"
  - "8 boneless pork chops, ¾-inch thick"
  - "4 large Granny Smith apples, sliced"
  - "2 large onions, sliced"
---

Mix soup, water, brown sugar, Worcestershire and thyme in 3 ½-quart slow cooker. Add pork, apples and onions.

Cover and cook on low 8 – 9 hours or on high 4 – 5 hours, or until pork is no longer pink.
