roll pan. Melt butter. Add sugar and nuts. Boil for 3 minutes, stirring frequently. Spread mixture on each cracker. Bake at 300º for 12 minutes. Remove from pan. Cool on wax paper. Watch the cooking. Don’t let it get too thick. Use a small spoon or fork to spread mixture on crackers.
