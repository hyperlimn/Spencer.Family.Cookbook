1 cup sweet butter 1 tsp. baking soda 1 ½ cups sugar 1 tsp. baking powder 2 egg yolks 1 cup sour cream 1 tsp. Vanilla 5 cups all-purpose flour

Cream butter and sugar until fluffy. Beat in egg yolks; then add remaining ingredients, except flour. Gradually add flour until well mixed. (Add 3 cups first and use mixer; then add last 2 cups and use large wooden spoon instead of mixer. It is too hard on the mixer.)

Roll dough into 1/8-inch thickness on lightly floured surface. Cut into desired shapes. Place ½-inch apart on lightly greased cookie sheets.

Bake 15 minutes at 350º. Remove from oven and from cookie sheet before cookie cools! Other- wise they will stick, even if you use Pam spray!

Frosting ½ stick butter 5 Tbsp. Carnation® evaporated milk ½ tsp. vanilla flavoring ½ tsp. orange (or favorite) flavoring Pinch of salt 2-3 cups powdered sugar

Mix all ingredients together except powdered sugar. Add sugar slowly, stirring constantly. Consistency should be like honey. Add more Sugar, or less, depending on consistency. Sprinkle “white sugar crystals” immediately on hot frosting as you frost cookies, if desired.

Absolutely delicious!
