ingredients:
  - "8-12 pieces of cubed steak"
  - "1 can Campbell’s® Golden Mushroom soup"
  - "1-2 soup cans water"
  - "1 small onion, sliced"
  - "Salt & pepper according to taste"
---

Roll steak in flour and brown in light olive oil until golden brown. Place in baking dish and add Campbell's Golden Mushroom Soup, mixed with 1 can of water. Pour over steak. Place sliced onions on top. Bake at 375º for 1½ hours, until tender.

Note: The soup and water makes a gravy as it bakes. Use 2 cans of water to make more gravy, especially if you use 12 pieces of steak.
