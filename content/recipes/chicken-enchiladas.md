ingredients:
  - "1 pkg. fresh corn tortillas"
  - "2 – 3 lbs. cooked chicken breasts, boned (or boneless)"
  - "1 cup chicken broth"
  - "1 can green chilies"
  - "1 lb. grated extra sharp cheddar cheese"
  - "1 can cream of mushroom soup"
  - "2 – 3 Tbsp. butter"
  - "1 medium onion, chopped"
  - "Sliced jalapeno peppers & black olives, optional"
---

Brown onion in butter & combine with soups, broth, green chilies & chicken. Line 9-x 13-inch baking dish with tortillas. Layer chicken mixture, grated cheese, tortillas, and cheese. Top with peppers and olives, 1 – 4 Tbsp. of each, if desired. Bake at 350º for 30 minutes.
