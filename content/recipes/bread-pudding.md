ingredients:
  - "10 – 15 slices of day-old French bread"
  - "½ cup butter, room temperature"
  - "8 eggs, beaten"
  - "1 cup granulated sugar"
  - "8 cups milk"
  - "2 Tbsp. vanilla"
---

Brown sugar Nutmeg Cinnamon

Lightly spread top surface of bread slices with butter. Lay in buttered 9-x 13-inch baking dish. Combine eggs, sugar, milk, vanilla. Mix together with a wire whisk or a blender. Pour mixture over bread. The bread may not absorb all the liquid. Save the extra egg, milk mixture. Refrigerate for 4 hours or overnight. Add the reserved liquid until all is absorbed. Sprinkle bread pudding generously with brown sugar, nutmeg, and cinnamon. Bake in a preheated 325º-350º oven for 1 hour until pudding is puffed and golden and knife inserted comes out clean.
