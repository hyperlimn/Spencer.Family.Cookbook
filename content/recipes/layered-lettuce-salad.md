Layer in the following order:

Lettuce, torn (medium sized head) Onions, chopped Water chestnuts, sliced (optional) Box frozen peas, uncooked 2 cups Hellmann’s® mayonnaise Tomatoes, sliced thinly 5 – 6 hard boiled eggs, grated Salt & pepper to taste Bacon bits (real bacon crumbles) Shredded cheddar cheese

Cover and place in refrigerator for 24 hours. May also use 1½ cups mayonnaise and 1½ cups Hidden Valley® Ranch Dressing in place of the 2 cups mayonnaise.
