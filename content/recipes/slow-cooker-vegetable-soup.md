ingredients:
  - "1 lb. short ribs or chuck roast *and soup bone"
  - "1 lb. beef stew meat cut into bite-sized pieces"
  - "1 (14 ½ oz. ) can tomatoes, diced with liquid"
  - "3 stalks celery with leaves"
  - "2 medium onions, diced"
  - "2 medium potatoes, diced"
  - "6 cups beef broth (I use “Better than Bouillon” (soup"
  - "base)"
  - "1 tsp. salt"
  - "Pepper to taste"
  - "¼ cabbage, diced (¼ head); a little goes a long way)"
  - "1 (10 oz.) pkg. frozen mixed vegetables"
  - "Add parsley"
  - "Add 1 Tbsp. sugar"
  - "Sprinkle crushed red pepper"
  - "1 pkg. lima beans (add 2 hours before finished)"
---

Put all ingredients in slow cooker and cook 8 — 10 hours on high or until short ribs are tender and fall off the bones.
