ingredients:
  - "2 lbs. sausage, uncooked"
  - "1 ½ cups Bisquick®"
  - "16 oz. sharp cheddar cheese (4 cups shredded)"
  - "½ cup finely chopped onion"
  - "½ cup finely chopped celery"
  - "½ tsp. garlic powder or 2 tsp. minced garlic"
  - "Black pepper"
---

Preheat oven to 375º. Shred cheese and mix all ingredients. Form into 1-inch balls. Bake 15 minutes on ungreased cookie sheet until golden brown.

Note: These can be frozen uncooked and baked later. Thaw for 15 minutes; bake 20 — 25 minutes.

For a firmer texture; add an extra 3 cups of Bisquik® and an extra 2 cups shredded cheese to the ingredients listed above.
