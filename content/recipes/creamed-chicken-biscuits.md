Boil chicken pieces (6 thighs, 8 drum sticks, and 4 chicken breasts with skin.)

Cook until meat is tender and has no pink color. (About 45 minutes to an hour on medium heat. The chicken should fall off the bone.)

De-bone chicken pieces, shred chicken, and place back in the chicken broth made from cooking.

Mix in a bowl: 2 cups of flour and add in cold water until it makes a paste (not too thin.) Stir flour mixture slowly into simmering chicken pieces and broth until broth thickens. Serve over your favorite biscuits.

Can also use canned chicken and put in College Inn chicken broth — about 42 oz.

Kids love this!
