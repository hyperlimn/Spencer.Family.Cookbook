ingredients:
  - "1 lb. pork sausage"
  - "6 eggs"
  - "1 tsp. dry mustard"
  - "6 slices white bread"
  - "3 cups milk"
  - "1 tsp. salt"
  - "1 ½ cups grated cheddar cheese"
---

Brown sausage and drain on paper towel. Beat eggs, milk, and other ingredients in bowl. Break bread into small pieces and mix with liquid mixture. Add drained sausage. Pour into greased 9-x 13-inch dish. Cover and refrigerate overnight. Bake at 350º for 1 hour or until firm. Serve hot. Cut into squares.
