ingredients:
  - "2 cans cream of chicken soup"
  - "2/3 cup apple juice"
  - "5 – 6 chicken breasts"
  - "Monterey jack cheese"
  - "Croutons"
---

Mix 1 can cream of chicken soup and 1/3 cup apple juice in casserole dish. Place chicken breasts in next and poke holes in chicken with fork. Mix 1 can cream of chicken soup and 1/3 cup apple juice together and pour over chicken.

Bake at 350º for 30 minutes. Remove chicken and place cheese over each breast; cook for 10 more minutes. Pull chicken out again and sprinkle with croutons. Cook for an additional 5 minutes.
