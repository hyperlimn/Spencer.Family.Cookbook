ingredients:
  - "4 cups coleslaw blend"
  - "2 cups sugar snap peas"
  - "2 large red peppers cut into strips"
  - "½ cup green onion slices"
  - "¼ cup dry roasted peanuts"
  - "1 can (15.25 oz.) tropical fruit salad, drained"
  - "½ cup Kraft® Zesty Italian Dressing"
---

Combine all ingredients except dressing in large bowl. Add dressing; toss to coat.

Serve immediately.
