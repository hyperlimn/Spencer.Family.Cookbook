Stir together until sugar is dissolved.

Add 6 oz. can of frozen orange juice and mix according to directions. Add 2 cans of crushed pineapple Add 8 bananas, sliced Mix and freeze until frosty.

To serve: Set slush out 30 minutes to 1 hour before serving.

Good for breakfast or as an addition to any meal.
