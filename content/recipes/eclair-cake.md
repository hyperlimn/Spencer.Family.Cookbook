Butter bottom of 9-x 13-inch pan. Line with whole graham crackers. Mix pudding with milk; beat at medium speed for only 2 minutes . Blend in whipped topping.

Pour half of mixture over graham crackers. Add second layer of graham crackers and pour on remaining pudding. Add 3 layer of graham crackers.
