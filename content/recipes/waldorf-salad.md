ingredients:
  - "4 cups cubed apples, Granny Smith & Fuji"
  - "2 cups canned pineapple tidbits, drained or fresh"
  - "pineapple"
  - "1 cup chopped celery"
  - "½cup mayonnaise"
  - "¼ cup whipping cream"
  - "1 Tbsp. powdered sugar"
  - "1 drop vanilla extract"
  - "½ cup chopped walnuts"
---

Combine apples, pineapple, celery, & mayonnaise. Seedless grapes and maraschino cherries make a nice addition. Toss & coat evenly.

Beat whipping cream until it has stiff peaks. Blend in sugar & vanilla. Fold into fruit mixture & chill.
