ingredients:
  - "1 lb. ground turkey (or lean ground beef)"
  - "1 pkg. (6 oz.) Stove Topº Stuffing mix"
  - "1 cup water"
  - "1 tsp. Italian Seasoning"
  - "½ cup spaghetti sauce"
  - "¼-½ cup shredded mozzarella cheese"
---

1 cup tomato juice ( I add a little more for moisture)

Preheat oven to 375°. Mix meat, stuffing mix, water, and seasoning until well blended. Press evenly into 12 muffin cups sprayed with cooking spray; make indentation in center of each with back of spoon. Spoon sauce evenly into indentations.

Bake 30 minutes or until meat loaves are cooked through (160° F). Top evenly with cheese; Make corn bread according to package directions. ( I continue baking 5 minutes or until cheese is melted. Let stand 10 minutes before serving.

Note: You may use your favorite meat loaf recipe and follow the same procedure.
