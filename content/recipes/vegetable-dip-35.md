ingredients:
  - "2 (8 oz.) sour cream"
  - "1 cup mayonnaise"
  - "2 tsp. dill weed"
  - "2 tsp. seasoned salt"
  - "2 tsp. onion flakes"
  - "Raw vegetables, cut into bite-sized pieces"
---

Stir together the sour cream, mayonnaise, dill weed, seasoned salt, and onion flakes. Keep refrigerated. Serve with raw vegetables.
