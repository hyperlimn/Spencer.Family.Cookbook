ingredients:
  - "1 lb. hot sausage (bulk)"
  - "1 small jar Cheese Whiz®"
  - "3 cups Bisquick® mix"
  - "½ cup milk"
---

Combine all ingredients. Shape into 1-inch balls. Place on ungreased cookie sheet. Bake at 400º for 15 – 20 minutes. May be prepared ahead and frozen.
