ingredients:
  - "1 tsp. mustard"
  - "½ cup mayonnaise"
  - "¾ cup shredded cheese (half Swiss & half cheddar)"
  - "½ cup chopped walnuts"
  - "8 bacon strips, fried, drained, crumbled"
  - "¼ cup chopped onion"
---

Mix all ingredients together. Spread on party rye bread. Bake for 10 – 15 minutes at 350º
