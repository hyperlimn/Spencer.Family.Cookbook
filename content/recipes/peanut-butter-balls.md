ingredients:
  - "2 cups creamy peanut butter"
  - "1 lb. butter"
  - "2 lbs. powdered sugar"
  - "3 tsp. vanilla"
  - "12 oz. semisweet chocolate morsels"
  - "¼ cup paraffin (canning wax)"
---

Mix first 4 ingredients until smooth. Chill.

Meanwhile, put chips and wax in double boiler until melted. Stir until smooth.

Roll peanut butter into walnut sized balls and dip into chocolate mix. You may use a toothpick inserted into the ball or 2 spoons rolling it into the chocolate mixture.

Place on wax paper until chocolate sets. Place in refrigerator until ready to serve.

Note : If peanut butter mixture is too cold, it will not coat properly.
