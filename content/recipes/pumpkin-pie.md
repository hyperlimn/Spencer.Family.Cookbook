ingredients:
  - "2 eggs, slightly beaten"
  - "1 ½ cups pumpkin"
  - "¾ cup sugar (½cup white & ¼ cup brown)"
  - "1 tsp. cinnamon"
  - "½ tsp. ginger"
  - "¼ tsp. ground cloves"
  - "1 2/3 cup Carnation® evaporated milk"
  - "1 (9-inch) pie shell"
---

Mix filling ingredients in order given. Bake in preheated 425º oven for 15 minutes. Reduce temperature to 350º and continue baking for 45 minutes. Check for doneness by inserting knife. It will come out clean. You can use a deep dish.
