Stir and bring to boil over medium heat and boil for 8-10 minutes or until mixture forms a soft ball. Keep stirring so that milk does not scorch.

Remove from heat and place in mixing bowl and add 3 heaping serving spoons ( long-handled type) which equals about 2/3 of a 24 oz. jar of Jif® peanut butter. (Jif® makes it creamy.)

Mix until creamy and smooth and place in a butter-greased glass dish. Let cool.
