ingredients:
  - "2 small sweet onions, chopped"
  - "1 ½ cups mayonnaise"
  - "1 ½ cups (6 oz.) shredded Swiss cheese"
  - "Assorted crackers"
---

In a large bowl, combine the onions, mayonnaise, and cheese. Spoon into an ungreased 9-inch pie plate. Bake, uncovered, at 350º for 25 – 30 minutes. Broil 6 inches from the heat for 2 – 3 minutes or until bubbly. Serve with crackers.
