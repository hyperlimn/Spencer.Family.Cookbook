ingredients:
  - "5 Tbsp. olive oil"
  - "2 Tbsp. plus 2 tsp. raspberry vinegar"
  - "3 to 4 tsp. sugar"
  - "2 tsp. chopped green onion"
  - "¼ tsp. poppy seeds"
  - "1/8 tsp. salt"
  - "1/8 tsp. pepper"
---

In a jar with a tight fitting lid, combine the vinaigrette ingredients; shake well. Drizzle over salad; toss to coat.
