Bake at 350 º for 30 minutes or until done. Don’t over bake. Cool, frost, and refrigerate.

Icing: Blend together until smooth: 1 stick butter 1 (8 oz.) pkg. cream cheese Add 2 cups powdered sugar & blend until smooth. Add 1 (8 oz.) tub Cool Whip. Frost & sprinkle with white sparkle sugar. Incredible!
