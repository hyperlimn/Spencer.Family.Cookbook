Mix together with spoon, not mixer. Bake at 350º in 9-x13-inch pan for 45 minutes.

Icing: 8 oz. cream cheese ½ cup butter 1 tsp. vanilla 1 ½ cups sifted confectioners’ sugar

Spread icing over cooled cake. Keep refrigerated.
