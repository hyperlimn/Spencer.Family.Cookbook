ingredients:
  - "1 cup light brown sugar"
  - "½ cup butter, divided"
  - "½ cup light corn syrup"
  - "2 Tbsp. heavy cream"
  - "½ tsp. vanilla"
  - "1/8 tsp. salt"
---

In a small, heavy non-aluminum saucepan, combine brown sugar and ¼ cup butter. Bring to boil over moderate heat. Whisk in the corn syrup, cream, vanilla, salt. Reduce the heat to moderately low and boil gently for about 3 minutes, stirring constantly. Immediately remove from heat and whisk the remaining ¼ cup butter. Serve warm.
