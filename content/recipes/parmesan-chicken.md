ingredients:
  - "4 boneless, skinless chicken breast"
  - "½ cup seasoned bread crumbs"
  - "¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese"
  - "½ tsp. dried basil"
  - "1 egg"
  - "1 Tbsp. butter"
  - "1 Tbsp. vegetable oil"
  - "Flatten chicken to ¼-inch thickness. In a shallow"
  - "bowl, combine bread crumbs, Parmesan and basil."
  - "In another bowl, beat the egg. Dip chicken into"
  - "egg; then coat with crumb mixture."
---

In a large skillet, brown chicken in butter and oil over medium heat for 3 – 5 minutes on each side or until juices run clear.
