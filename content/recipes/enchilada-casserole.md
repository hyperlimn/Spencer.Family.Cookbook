Drain and add: 1 can cream of mushroom soup 1 can cream of chicken soup 1 small can enchilada sauce 1 can chopped green chilies

Simmer for 10 minutes. Layer 1 bag of nacho chips in 9-x 13-inch pan. Pour mixture on top; cover with 2 cups of shredded cheddar cheese. Bake at 350° for approximately 30 minutes.
