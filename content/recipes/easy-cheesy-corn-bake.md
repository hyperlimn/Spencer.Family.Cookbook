ingredients:
  - "4 oz. cream cheese, softened"
  - "¾ cup milk"
  - "3 eggs, beaten"
  - "1 can (15½ oz.) whole kernel corn, drained"
  - "1 can cream-style corn"
  - "1pkg. (½ oz.) corn muffin mix"
  - "1 cup shredded cheddar cheese"
---

Preheat oven to 375º. Whisk cream cheese and milk until smooth. Stir in remaining ingredients until well blended.

Pour into 9-x 13-inch baking dish sprayed with cooking spray.

Bake 35 – 36 minutes or until golden brown.
