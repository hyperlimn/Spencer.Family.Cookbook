Mix together with spoon:

2 cups sugar 4 tsp. salt 4 tsp. dry mustard

Mix with mixer:

4 cups vegetable oil 1 1/3 cups red wine vinegar

Stir in 4 tsp. celery seeds & the minced onions. Refrigerate.
