Place 1 oz. cut up Velveeta cheese and 2 Tbsp. salsa in small microwavable bowl.

Microwave on high for 30 seconds or until cheese is melted, stirring after 15 seconds.

Stir until blended.

Serve with Wheat Thins Crackers for dipping.
