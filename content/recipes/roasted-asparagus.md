ingredients:
  - "4 lbs. fresh asparagus, trimmed"
  - "¼ cup olive oil"
  - "½ tsp. salt"
  - "¼ tsp. pepper"
  - "¼ cup sesame seeds, toasted"
---

Arrange asparagus in a single layer in two foil-lined 15-inch x 10-inch x 1-inch baking pans.

Drizzle with oil.

Sprinkle with salt and pepper.

Bake, uncovered, at 400º for 12 – 15 minutes or until crisp-tender, turning once.

Sprinkle with sesame seeds, if desired.

Note: You may also use this with fresh broccoli. I use garlic salt and sprinkle with Parmesan cheese. Delicious!
