Brown 1 lb. of ground meat (hamburger or sausage) in 2 Tbsp. of olive oil.

Sprinkle over browned meat 1/3 cup flour; stir and add 1 can of Carnation® evaporated milk and 1 can of water. Salt and pepper to taste; add crushed red pepper for some “zip.” Serve over biscuits or toast.

Note: May need to add extra milk to get desired consistency for gravy. Cook over medium heat and let simmer until you reach desired consistency.
