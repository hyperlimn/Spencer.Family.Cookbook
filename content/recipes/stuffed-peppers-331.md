1 lb. extra lean ground beef 1 can (8 oz.) tomato sauce 1 can (15 oz.) Spanish rice 1 large onion, chopped 1/2 tsp. salt 1/4 tsp. black pepper 4 large bell peppers, tops removed, cleaned of seeds and cored, but not halved.

Combine all ingredients except half can of tomato sauce and bell peppers.

Stuff bell peppers with mixture.

Place peppers in slow cooker. Pour remaining tomato sauce over peppers.

Cover and cook on high for 4 hours.
