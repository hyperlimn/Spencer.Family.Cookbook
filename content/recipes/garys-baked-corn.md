ingredients:
  - "1 can cream style corn"
  - "1 can whole kernel corn, drained"
  - "1 Tbsp. sugar"
  - "2 Tbsp. minced onion"
  - "2 eggs, beaten slightly"
  - "½ cup milk"
  - "1/3 cup cracker crumbs"
---

Mix all ingredients and bake at 325º for 1 hour or until knife comes out clean.
