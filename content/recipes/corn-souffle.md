ingredients:
  - "1 can whole kernel corn"
  - "1 can cream style corn"
  - "1 cup sour cream"
  - "2 eggs, beaten"
  - "1 stick butter, melted"
  - "1 box Jiffy® corn muffin mix"
  - "Onion powder"
  - "Salt & pepper to taste"
---

Mix all ingredients.

Bake in 2-quart casserole for 45 minutes at 350º.
