ingredients:
  - "3 cups torn fresh spinach"
  - "1 can (11 oz.) mandarin oranges, drained"
  - "½ cup sliced fresh mushrooms"
  - "3 thin slices red onion, halved"
  - "½ lb. boneless, skinless chicken breasts cut into"
  - "strips"
  - "¼ cup chopped walnuts"
  - "2 Tbsp. olive oil"
  - "2 tsp. cornstarch"
  - "½ tsp. ground ginger"
  - "¼ cup orange juice"
  - "¼ cup red wine vinegar or cider vinegar"
---

On two salad plates, arrange spinach, oranges, mushrooms, and onion; set aside. In a skillet, sauté chicken and walnuts in oil until chicken is no longer pink. In a small bowl, combine the cornstarch, ginger, orange juice, and vinegar until smooth; stir into the chicken mixture. Bring to a boil; cook and stir for 2 minutes or until thickened and bubbly. Pour over salads and serve immediately.
