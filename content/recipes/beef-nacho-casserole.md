ingredients:
  - "1 lb. ground beef"
  - "1 jar (2 oz.) chunky salsa"
  - "¾ cup Miracle Whip"
  - "1 Tbsp. chili powder"
  - "2 cups crushed tortilla chips, divided"
  - "2 cups (8 oz.) shredded Colby/Monterey Jack"
  - "Cheese, divided"
---

Heat oven to 350º. Brown meat and onion; drain. Stir in salsa, Miracle Whip, and chili powder. Layer ½ each of the meat mixture, chips, and cheese in 2-quart casserole. Repeat layers. Bake 20 minutes or until thoroughly heated. Top with shredded lettuce and chopped tomato, if desired.
