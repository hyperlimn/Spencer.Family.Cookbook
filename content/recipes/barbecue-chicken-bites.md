ingredients:
  - "1 egg"
  - "2 Tbsp. milk"
  - "4 cups barbecued potato chips, crushed"
  - "½ lb. boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into"
  - "1½-inch cubes"
  - "Barbecue sauce:"
---

In a shallow bowl, whisk egg and milk. Place potato chips in another shallow bowl. Dip chicken in egg mixture; then roll in chips. Place in a single layer on a greased baking sheet. Bake at 400º for 10 – 15 minutes or until juices run clear. Serve with barbecue sauce.
