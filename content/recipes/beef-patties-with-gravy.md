ingredients:
  - "1 egg"
  - "½ cup soft bread crumbs"
  - "1 Tbsp. finely chopped onion"
  - "½ lb. lean ground beef"
  - "1 can (10¾ oz.) condensed Golden Mushroom"
  - "soup, undiluted"
  - "2/3 cup water"
  - "½ cup sliced fresh mushrooms (optional)"
  - "Hot cooked noodles, rice, or mashed potatoes"
---

In a bowl, combine the egg, bread crumbs and onion. Crumble beef over mixture and mix well. Shape into two patties. In a skillet, cook patties until browned on both sides. In a bowl, combine the soup, water, and mushrooms; pour over patties. Bring to a boil. Reduce heat; cover and simmer until meat is no longer pink. Serve over noodles, rice, or potatoes.
