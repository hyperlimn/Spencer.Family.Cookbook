Bake at 375º for 20-22 minute, or until cake is golden brown and tests done. Cool for 20 minutes. Combine ½ cup butter and milk in saucepan; bring to a boil. Remove from heat. Mix in sugar and ½ tsp. almond extract. Stir in nuts. Frost warm cake.
