ingredients:
  - "1½ cups butter 2 Tbsp. lukewarm milk"
  - "3 Tbsp. sugar 3 well-beaten egg yolks"
  - "4 cups flour ¾ cup sour cream"
  - "½ tsp. salt 1 lg. cake yeast ( dairy section)"
---

Take cake yeast & crumble in lukewarm milk.

Cream butter and sugar; add flour, salt, yeast mixture, egg yolks, and sour cream.

Mix dough all together with hands until soft. Leave in bowl and chill overnight.

Next Day: Divide dough into 8 balls, working with one at a time. Leave the rest in the refrigerator until ready to roll out.

Roll each dough ball out, one at a time, on sugar into a circle. Continued...

Spread nut mixture thinly on top. Do not spread all the way to the edges; leave ½-inch edge all around.

Cut into 16 pieces with pizza cutter. Roll from wide end to skinny end. Place on ungreased cookie sheet. Bake at 350° for 20 minutes.

Nut Filling: 1½ lbs. ground walnuts (measure 6 cups & grind) 3 egg whites ¾ cup honey ½ cup white sugar

Mix together. It will be thick. Add a little tiny bit of milk to thin out.

Fabulous!
