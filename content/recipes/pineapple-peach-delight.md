1 (20 oz.) can crushed pineapple in own juice 2 bags frozen peaches 1 box white cake mix Raisins 1 stick butter, melted

Place crushed pineapple in juice in 9-x 13-inch pan. Place 1 bag frozen peaches on top.

Sprinkle box of dry white cake mix over peaches.

Place 2 bag of frozen peaches over that.

Sprinkle with raisins.

Melt 1 stick of butter and drizzle over cake.

Bake at 350° for 55-60 minutes.

Let cool for 5 minutes.

Top with ice cream & sprinkle with pecans.
