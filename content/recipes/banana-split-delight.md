Mix together to make crust. Bake 4 – 8 minutes at 350º.

Using electric mixer, mix in bowl for 15 minutes: 1 lb. box powdered sugar 1 tsp. vanilla 2 eggs, beaten (can substitute 150 ml. of milk or 2 bananas but changes consistency) 1 stick butter (room temperature)

Pour ingredients over chilled crust.

Then pour on 2 cans crushed pineapple, drained well. Reserve syrup. Slice 4 bananas and dip in reserved juice. Place over pineapple. Top with layer of whipped topping. Garnish with walnuts or pecans (if desired) and maraschino cherries. Chocolate syrup drizzled on top is optional.
