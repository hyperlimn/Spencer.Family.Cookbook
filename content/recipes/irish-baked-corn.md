ingredients:
  - "1 small onion, chopped"
  - "1 stick butter"
  - "16 oz. whole corn, drained"
  - "16 oz. creamed corn"
  - "16 oz. sour cream"
  - "2 boxes Jiffy® corn muffin mix"
  - "Salt, pepper, and garlic to taste (optional)"
---

Mix first five ingredients. Add muffin mix. Pour into a greased 9-x13-inch pan. Bake 1 hour at 350° until set.
