  - "Pastry for 9-inch one-crust pie"
  - "2 eggs"
  - "½ cup all – purpose flour"
  - "½ cup sugar"
  - "½ cup firmly packed brown sugar"
  - "**1 cup butter, melted and cooled to room"
  - "temperature"
  - "One 6-oz. pkg. (1 cup) Nestle® Toll House"
  - "semisweet chocolate morsels"
  - "1 cup chopped walnuts"
---

Preheat oven to 325º. Line pie plate with pastry shell. In large bowl, beat eggs until foamy. Add flour, sugar and brown sugar. Beat until well blended. Blend in butter. Stir in chocolate morsels and nuts. Pour into pastry. Bake until set, about 1 hour. Serve with whipped cream or ice cream, if desired.

**Note: Melt butter and then cool to room temperature (not hot at all) or it will be mushy.
