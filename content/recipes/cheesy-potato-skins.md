ingredients:
  - "3 medium baking potatoes"
  - "Vegetable oil"
  - "Seasoned salt"
  - "1 cup (4 oz.) shredded cheddar cheese"
  - "6 slices bacon, fried & crumbled"
  - "Sour cream"
  - "Scrub potatoes thoroughly and rub skins with oil."
---

Bake at 400º for 1 hour or until done. Allow potatoes to cool to touch. Cut in half lengthwise; carefully scoop out pulp leaving ¼-inch shell. Deep fry in hot oil (375º) for 2 minutes or until lightly browned. Drain on paper towels. Place skins on baking sheet; sprinkle with salt, cheese, and bacon. Place under broiler until cheese melts. Serve with sour cream.
