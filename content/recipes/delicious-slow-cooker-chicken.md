ingredients:
  - "4 lbs. Chicken breasts"
  - "1 pkg. Italian dressing mix, dry"
  - "4 medium carrots"
  - "4 medium potatoes"
  - "2 onions, quartered"
  - "Make Italian dressing as directed on package."
---

Place chicken and vegetables in slow cooker, placing vegetables on bottom. Pour dressing over meat and vegetables. Cook on high for 4 hours or on low for 8 hours.

Note: Can substitute 8 oz. of bottled Italian dressing
