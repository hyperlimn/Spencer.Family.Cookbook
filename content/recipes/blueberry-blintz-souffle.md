ingredients:
  - "3 Tbsp. butter, melted"
  - "2 pkgs. (13 oz. each) frozen blueberry blintzes"
  - "4 eggs, lightly beaten"
  - "1 ½ cups (12 oz.) sour cream"
  - "¼ cup sugar"
  - "1 Tbsp. orange juice"
  - "1 tsp. vanilla extract"
  - "Confectioners’ sugar, optional"
---

Place butter in a 9-x 13-inch baking dish. Top with blintzes. In a bowl, combine the eggs, sour cream, sugar, orange juice, and vanilla. Pour over blintzes.

Cover and bake at 350º for 55 minutes. Uncover; bake 10 – 15 minutes longer or until lightly browned and a knife inserted in the egg mixture comes out clean. Sprinkle with confectioners’ sugar if desired.
