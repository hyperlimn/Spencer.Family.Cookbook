ingredients:
  - "1 ½ cups brown sugar"
  - "1 cup butter or shortening"
  - "2 ½ cups flour"
  - "1 tsp. baking soda"
  - "3 eggs"
  - "1 ½ Tbsp. cinnamon"
  - "1 Tbsp. cloves"
  - "1 cup nuts"
  - "1 cup raisins"
---

Cream sugar and shortening. Add eggs. Add flour, soda, and spices. Add 1 cup nuts and 1 cup raisins. Drop by teaspoonful on cookie sheet. Bake at 375º for 10 minutes.
