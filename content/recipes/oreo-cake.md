Mix 2 packages cream cheese, 2 cups confectioners’ sugar, and 1 container whipped topping. Spread over cookies.

Beat 2 small boxes of chocolate instant pudding and 3 cups cold milk until thick. Spread over cream cheese mixture.

Ice with whipped topping and sprinkle with mini chocolate chips or chocolate shavings. Refrigerate.
