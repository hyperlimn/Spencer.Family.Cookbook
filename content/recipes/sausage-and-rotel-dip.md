ingredients:
  - "1 lb. mild pork sausage"
  - "1 (8 oz.) cream cheese"
  - "2 cans Rotel® diced tomatoes with green chilies"
  - "(drained)"
---

Brown and crumble sausage. Drain and return to skillet. Add cream cheese and heat on low until melted. Add Rotel® diced tomatoes and stir until blended. Serve warm with tortilla chips.
