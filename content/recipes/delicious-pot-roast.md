ingredients:
  - "3 – 4 lb. beef round or chuck roast"
  - "1 pkg. Lipton onion soup mix, dry"
  - "1 – 2 cups water"
  - "6 medium potatoes"
  - "6 carrots"
---

Place meat, vegetables, soup mix , and water in slow cooker, placing vegetables on bottom. Cook on high for 4 hours or on low for 8 hours.
