  - "*1 can (14½ oz.) chicken broth"
  - "2 cups Minute Brown Rice, uncooked"
  - "3 cups broccoli florets"
  - "4 bone-in pork chops, ½-inch thick"
  - "*¼ cup Catalina dressing"
  - "¼ cup orange juice"
  - "3 Tbsp. maple-flavored or pancake syrup"
  - "2 Tbsp. sliced almonds, toasted"
  - "Bring broth to boil in medium saucepan on medium-high"
  - "heat; stir in rice. Cover. Reduce heat to medium low;"
  - "simmer 3 minutes. Add broccoli; stir. Cover; simmer an"
  - "additional 2 minutes. Remove from heat, let stand 5"
  - "minutes or until broth is completely absorbed."
  - "Meanwhile, heat large nonstick skillet sprayed with"
  - "cooking spray on medium heat. Add chops; cook 4"
  - "minutes on each side or until lightly browned on both"
  - "sides and cooked through. Remove from skillet; cover to"
  - "keep warm."
  - "Add dressing, orange juice, and syrup to skillet. Bring to"
  - "boil on medium-high heat; cook 3 minutes or until sauce"
  - "is reduced by half. Serve chops and rice drizzled with the"
  - "sauce; sprinkle with almonds."
  - "**Note: May use fat-free, reduced sodium chicken broth and"
  - "Light Catalina dressing for a more healthy meal."
