Pour into buttered 9-x 13-inch pan.

Sweeten 2 – 3 cups desired fruit with sugar (blackberries, peaches, cherries). Pour over batter. Bake for 30 minutes at 425º. When serving, top with whipped cream. You may also bake the cake by itself. Then pour strawberries over like you would for a shortcake.
