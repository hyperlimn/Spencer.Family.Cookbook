Add: 2 cups cooked chicken, approximately 1 lb. ¼ tsp. salt 1/8 tsp. pepper 2 Tbsp. milk 1 Tbsp. chopped onion 1 Tbsp. chopped pimento, optional

Mix well. Separate 8 oz. can of refrigerated crescent rolls into 4 rectangles. Seal the perforations. Spoon ½ cup chicken mixture into center of each rectangle. Pull up the 4 corners over center of mixture and seal. Brush top with 1 Tbsp. melted butter and sprinkle with Parmesan cheese or crushed croutons. Bake at 350º for 20 – 25 minutes on an ungreased cookie sheet. Serves 4.
