ingredients:
  - "5 Tbsp. flour"
  - "¼ tsp. salt"
  - "2 cups milk"
  - "3 egg yolks"
  - "1 cup coconut"
  - "2 tsp. vanilla"
  - "½ cup sugar"
  - "1 Tbsp. butter"
---

Mix sugar, salt, flour; add egg yolks and milk. Cook for 10 minutes; add coconut & vanilla and pour into a baked pie shell. Top with whipped topping or meringue. Garnish with toasted, flaked coconut.
