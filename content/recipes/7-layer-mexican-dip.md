ingredients:
  - "1 (8 oz.) pkg. Cream cheese, softened"
  - "1 Tbsp. taco seasoning mix"
  - "1 cup each guacamole, salsa, and"
  - "shredded lettuce"
  - "1 cup (4 oz.) shredded sharp cheddar"
  - "cheese"
  - "½ cup chopped green onions"
  - "2 Tbsp. sliced, pitted ripe olives"
---

Mix cream cheese and seasoning mix. Spread on bottom of 9-inch pie plate or quiche dish. Layer remaining ingredients over cream cheese mixture. Refrigerate. Serve with tortilla chips.
