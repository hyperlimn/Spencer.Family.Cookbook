roll dough onto cookie sheet, pressing the edges to- gether to seal them, and pressing it up the sides of the cookie sheet. Bake at 375º for 5 to 11 minutes or until golden brown. Baking time will depend upon how thin the crust is. Watch it carefully so that it doesn’t burn. Cool. Mix together cream cheese, mayonnaise, and dry salad dressing. Spread on dough. Top with the chopped veggies and the shredded cheese. Cover and keep refrigerated. When ready to serve, cut into appetizer sized pieces.
