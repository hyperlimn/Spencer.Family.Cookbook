Melt together: 6 oz. unsweetened chocolate 1 cup butter

Add: 2 cups sugar 4 large eggs

Add: 1 cup flour 1 Tbsp. vanilla

Fold in: 2 cups mini marshmallows 1 cup chocolate chips Chopped nuts, optional

Spread in 9-x 13-inch pan, sprinkle with ½ cup chocolate chips on top. Bake at 300º for 53 – 55 minutes. Do not over bake. Cool. Wrap in foil or keep in container. Decadent!
