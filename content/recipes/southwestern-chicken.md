2 cans (15 oz. each) corn, undrained 1 can (15 oz.) black beans, rinsed and drained 1 jar (16 oz.) chunky salsa 6 boneless, skinless chicken breast halves 1 cup low-fat shredded Cheddar cheese

Combine corn, beans, and 1/2 cup of salsa in slow cooker.

Top with chicken. Pour remaining salsa over chicken.

Cover and cook on low 7-8 hours.
