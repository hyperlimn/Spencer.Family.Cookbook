1 box chocolate cake mix

Topping: White icing:

Bake cake following directions on the box. Create icing by creaming Crisco® and butter together. Add milk, vanilla, and salt. Gradually add in powdered sugar and beat for 10-15 minutes. Refrigerate until fluffy and spread over cake. Beat topping ingredients together and spread over white icing. (Icing should be thin enough to spread. If necessary, add water.) (You may need to add more butter & hot water to compensate for the egg or use liquid pasteurized eggs.) Keep refrigerated.
