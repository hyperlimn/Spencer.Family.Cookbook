Mix together: 1 (12 oz.) container whipped topping, thawed 1 (8 oz.) pkg. cream cheese 1 can Eagle Brand® Milk

Divide mixture into pie crusts and freeze.

When ready to serve, top with toasted coconut and pecans and drizzle with caramel sauce.
