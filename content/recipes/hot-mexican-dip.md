ingredients:
  - "1 (8 oz.) package cream cheese"
  - "1 (15 oz.) can refried beans"
  - "1 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese"
  - "1 Tbsp. taco seasoning mix"
  - "1 cup salsa"
  - "½ cup green onions, chopped"
  - "½ cup red pepper"
  - "2 Tbsp. sliced, ripe olives"
---

Mix cream cheese and seasoning mix. Spread on the bottom of a 9-inch pie plate or quiche dish. Layer remaining ingredients over cream cheese mixture. Bake at 350º for 20 minutes or until the center is bubbly. Garnish with lettuce and cherry tomatoes. Serve with tortilla chips.

One of John’s favorites!
