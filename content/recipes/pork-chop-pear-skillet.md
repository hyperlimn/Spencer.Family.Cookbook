ingredients:
  - "2 Tbsp. pineapple juice"
  - "2 Tbsp. honey"
  - "2 Tbsp. water, divided"
  - "1 ½ tsp. lemon juice"
  - "Dash ground ginger"
  - "1 medium ripe pear, sliced"
  - "¾ tsp. cornstarch"
  - "2 bone-in pork chops (1/2-inch thick)"
  - "1 Tbsp. vegetable oil"
  - "1/8 tsp. salt"
  - "Dash pepper"
---

In a small bowl, combine the pineapple juice, honey, 1 Tbsp. water, lemon juice, cornstarch, and ginger. Stir in pear slices; set aside. In a skillet over medium-high heat, brown pork chops in oil on both sides. Sprinkle with salt and pepper. Reduce heat. Pour pear mixture over chops. Bring to a boil. Reduce heat; cover and simmer for 3 – 4 minutes or until meat juices run clear.
