ingredients:
  - "1 lb. ground beef"
  - "¼ tsp. grated onion"
  - "1 can mushroom or cream of chicken soup, undiluted"
  - "1 small can mushrooms, optional"
  - "1 cup sour cream"
  - "3 Tbsp. flour"
  - "Salt & pepper to taste"
  - "Garlic, to taste (powder, salt, or minced)"
---

Brown ground beef & onion in olive oil. Add flour & simmer for 5 minutes. Season to taste. Stir in soup & mushrooms. Simmer for 10 minutes, uncovered. Stir in sour cream and heat through.

Serve over egg noodles.
