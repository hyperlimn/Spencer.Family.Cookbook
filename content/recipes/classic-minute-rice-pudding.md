ingredients:
  - "1¾ cups milk"
  - "½ cup Minute White Rice, uncooked"
  - "2 Tbsp. each sugar and raisins"
  - "¼ tsp. salt"
  - "1 egg"
  - "¼ tsp. vanilla"
---

Combine milk, rice, sugar, raisins, and salt in medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly. Reduce heat to medium-low; simmer, uncovered, 6 minutes, stirring occasionally.

Beat egg and vanilla with wire whisk until well blended. Add a small amount of the hot milk mixture; mix well. Return to saucepan; cook and stir on low. Heat one minute. (Do not boil.)

Remove from heat. Let stand 30 minutes. Serve warm. Store any remaining pudding in refrigerator.
