ingredients:
  - "1 (2 liter bottle) Raspberry Ginger Ale, chilled"
  - "1 bottle white grape juice, chilled"
  - "1 container Raspberry sherbet, softened just"
  - "a little"
---

Place very slightly softened sherbet in punch bowl. Pour grape juice over sherbet. Pour Raspberry Ginger Ale over the sherbet last. Serve cold.
