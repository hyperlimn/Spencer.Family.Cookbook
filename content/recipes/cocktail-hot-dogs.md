cut in fourths 1 (12 oz.) jar grape jelly 3 – 4 oz. yellow mustard 1 – 5 tbsp. Brown sugar

Mix jelly, mustard, and brown sugar together in a saucepan and cook over medium heat until the jelly is melted. Stir in the hot dog pieces and continue to cook over medium heat for about 30 minutes, stirring occasionally. Keep in slow cooker on low for serving.
