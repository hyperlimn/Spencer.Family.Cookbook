ingredients:
  - "28 oz. jar spaghetti sauce"
  - "½ cup grated Parmesan cheese"
  - "¼ cup Italian bread crumbs"
  - "6 – 8 boneless, skinless chicken breasts"
  - "3 – 6 Tbsp. butter, melted"
  - "1 cup (4 oz.) shredded mozzarella cheese"
---

Pour spaghetti sauce into jelly roll pan (15-x 10-inch) Combine Parmesan cheese and bread crumbs. Dip chicken in butter; coat with crumbs. Place chicken over spaghetti sauce in pan. Bake at 400º for 25 minutes. Top with mozzarella cheese. Bake an additional 5 minutes.

Serve over cooked pasta.
