ingredients:
  - "2 pkg. (4-serving size each) Jell-O® Pistachio"
  - "Flavor Instant Pudding"
  - "2 cans (20 oz. each) crushed pineapple in juice,"
  - "undrained"
  - "2 cups miniature marshmallows"
  - "1 cup chopped pecans"
  - "3 cups (8 oz.) container whipped topping"
---

Mix dry pudding mix, pineapple, marshmallows and pecans in large bowl until well blended. Gently stir in whipped topping; cover.

Refrigerate 1 hour or until ready to serve. Garnish with additional whipped topping and chopped pecans, if desired.
