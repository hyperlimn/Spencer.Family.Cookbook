3 – 4 slices bacon, cut into pieces. Place in 3-quart casserole. Microwave on high for 3 minutes.

With slotted spoon remove bacon and place on paper towel to drain.

To bacon drippings add:

¼ cup vinegar 2 tsp. sugar ¼ tsp. salt 1/8 tsp. pepper 1/8 tsp. crushed dried tarragon Chopped green onions

Microwave on high for 2 minutes or until it boils. Tear up leaf lettuce and add to drippings. Add crumbled bacon.
