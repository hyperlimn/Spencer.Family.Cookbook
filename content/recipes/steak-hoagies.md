ingredients:
  - "2# thin sirloin steak , cut into bite-sized"
  - "pieces"
  - "6 hoagie buns or sausage rolls (from bakery section)"
  - "3 red bell peppers"
  - "3 green bell peppers (I omit these)"
  - "4 Vidalia onions"
  - "Olive oil"
  - "American, provolone, or mozzarella cheese"
---

Place 1 Tbsp. olive oil in skillet and sauté peppers (red & green peppers must be sautéed separately). Do not salt them; sauté over medium heat; will need to cook for about 30 minutes., until soft and blackened on edges.

Sauté onion, sliced, in separate skillet with olive oil. Brown steak in olive oil until brown and tender. Season to taste as you are browning it. Slice buns and line with cheese slices. Place steak in bun. Wrap in aluminum foil and place on cookie sheet. Bake for 15 minutes at 350 º . Place peppers and onions on top as you choose. Delicious! Liz’s favorite!
