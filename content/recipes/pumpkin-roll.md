Cream until light and fluffy: 3 eggs

Add: 1 cup white sugar Fold in: 2/3 cup pumpkin 1 tsp. lemon juice Fold in: ¾ cup all-purpose flour 1 tsp. baking powder 2 tsp. pumpkin pie spice

Pour into well greased jelly roll pan lined with wax paper. Bake at 375º for 15 minutes. Turn out on linen towel and sprinkle with powdered sugar. Roll in towel. Let cool.
