ingredients:
  - "1 lb. skinless, boneless chicken breasts (4 breasts)"
  - "1 onion, chopped"
  - "1 small bag of baby carrots"
  - "4 potatoes, cut up"
  - "1 pkg. Knorr® vegetable soup mix*"
  - "1 (10 oz.) can cream of chicken soup"
  - "4 oz. water (I add more water)"
---

Place chicken and vegetables in slow cooker. Mix soup mix, chicken soup, and water; then pour over chicken and veggies. Cook on high approximately 4 hours.

*You can also substitute an onion soup mix for the Knorr® soup mix.
