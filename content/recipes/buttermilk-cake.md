ingredients:
  - "2 sticks butter"
  - "3 cups sugar"
  - "4 eggs"
  - "3 cups flour, sifted"
  - "½ tsp. baking soda"
  - "¼ tsp. salt"
  - "3 tsp. nutmeg"
  - "1 cup buttermilk"
---

Cream together butter and sugar. Add eggs and beat for 10 minutes. In separate bowl, mix flour, soda, salt, and nutmeg. Alternate flour mixture and buttermilk to sugar and egg mixture until well blended. Put into 2 medium-sized and 1 small, greased loaf pans. Bake at 325° for 1½ hours. Sprinkle sugar and nutmeg on top.
