ingredients:
  - "8 oz. cream cheese"
  - "3 – 4 Tbsp. green onions, tops only"
  - "1 pkg. chopped beef, smoked, sliced, pressed"
---

Chop up ½ package of the beef and onions. Soften cream cheese. Put all ingredients in mixer and mix together. Place in refrigerator for 10 minutes. Form into ball. Layer remaining beef in slices over cheese ball. Refrigerate. Serve with crackers.
