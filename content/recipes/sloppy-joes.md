  - "10 oz. jar India Relish"
  - "12 oz. jar chili sauce"
  - "20 oz. jar ketchup"
  - "1 onion, chopped"
  - "½ cup sugar"
  - "Salt to taste"
---

After browning and draining the beef, add everything else into a large pot. Bring to a boil and reduce heat and simmer for several hours, stirring every once in a while.
