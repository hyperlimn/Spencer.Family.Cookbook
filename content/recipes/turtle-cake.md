Put one half of cake mixture in 9-x 13-inch pan. Bake at 350 º for 6 minutes.

While the cake is baking, mix: 1/3 cup evaporated milk 1 pkg. caramels

Stir until smooth. Remove from heat. Sprinkle, while cake is hot, 12 oz. chocolate chips over cake. Then pour melted caramel over cake. Spread remaining cake mix over cake. Bake for 20 – 25 minutes at 350º
