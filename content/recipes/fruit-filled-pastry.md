ingredients:
  - "2 cans crescent rolls"
  - "1 can pie filling (apple, blueberry, peach,"
  - "cherry, or blackberry)"
  - "1 (8 oz.) pkg. cream cheese"
  - "¼ cup powdered sugar"
  - "1 egg"
  - "1 tsp. vanilla"
---

Arrange crescent rolls on baking stone with point toward the center. Mix cream cheese, powdered sugar, egg, and vanilla in a bowl. Spread cream cheese mixture over crescent rolls. Spread pie filling over cream mixture. Turn edges of crescent rolls toward the center. Bake for 20 – 25 minutes at 350º.
