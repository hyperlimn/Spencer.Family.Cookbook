  - "Chopped onion"
  - "2 large cans whole tomatoes, pureed in blender"
  - "2 – 3 Tbsp. chili powder"
  - "1 tsp. Red cayenne pepper"
  - "1 tsp. Black pepper"
  - "2 Tbsp. sugar"

Brown meat in olive oil; drain. Place into large kettle. Add kidney beans, drained; pureed toma- toes, and seasonings. Simmer on stove top for about 1 hour. Garnish with shredded cheese, sour cream, diced onions (optional).
