ingredients:
  - "1 2/3 cups hot water"
  - "1 package (6 oz.) Stove Top® Stuffing Mix for"
  - "Chicken"
  - "6 small boneless, skinless chicken breasts"
  - "2/3 cup orange juice"
  - "1/3 cup firmly packed brown sugar"
  - "3 Tbsp. butter, melted"
---

Preheat oven to 400º. Add hot water to stuffing mix; stir just until moistened. Set aside.

Place chicken in 9-x 13-inch dish. Mix juice, sugar, and butter until well blended; pour over chicken. Top with prepared stuffing.

Bake 30 minutes or until chicken is cooked through.
