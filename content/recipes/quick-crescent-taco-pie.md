ingredients:
  - "1 ¼ lbs. Lean ground beef Shredded lettuce"
  - "1 pkg. taco seasoning mix Sliced black olives"
  - "½ cup water Diced tomatoes"
  - "½ cup chunky salsa Diced onions"
  - "1 (8 oz.) tube crescent rolls"
  - "1½ cups crushed tortilla chips, divided"
  - "1 (8 oz.) carton sour cream"
  - "6 slices American cheese"
---

Brown meat in large skillet in 1 Tbsp. olive oil; drain. Add seasoning mix, water and salsa; simmer for 5 minutes. Spread the crescent roll dough in 10-inch pie plate (or deep dish) to form crust, pressing edges together at seams.

Sprinkle 1 cup tortilla chips on crust bottom, reserving remaining ½ cup. Spoon meat mixture on top of chips. Spread sour cream over meat. Cover with cheese slices; sprinkle on remaining ½ cup corn chips.

Bake at 375º for 20 minutes; until crust is golden brown. Serve with lettuce, olives, onions, and tomatoes. Another family favorite!
