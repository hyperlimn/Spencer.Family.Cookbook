  - "¾ tsp. salt"
  - "6 large egg whites"

In a small saucepan, melt butter over moderately low heat and continue to heat until golden brown with a nut-like fragrance. (Bottom of the pan will be covered with brown specks.) Cool butter to warm and stir in vanilla.

Preheat oven to 375º. Butter and flour a 9-inch round cake pan, knocking out excess flour.
