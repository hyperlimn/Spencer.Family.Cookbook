ingredients:
  - "6 large baking potatoes"
  - "½ cup butter, softened"
  - "¾ to 1 cup evaporated milk"
  - "3 Tbsp. crumbled cooked bacon"
  - "3 Tbsp. finely chopped onion"
  - "1 Tbsp. snipped chives"
  - "½ tsp. salt"
  - "Dash pepper"
  - "1½ cups (6 oz.) shredded cheddar cheese, divided"
  - "Paprika"
---

Bake potatoes at 400º for 60 minutes or until soft. Cut a lengthwise slice from top of the potatoes. Scoop out the pulp and place in a small mixing bowl. Mash potatoes and butter. Beat in milk or cream, bacon, onion, chives, salt, pepper, and 1 cup cheese.

Refill potato shells. Top with remaining cheese and sprinkle with paprika. Bake at 375º for 25 – 30 minutes or until heated through.
