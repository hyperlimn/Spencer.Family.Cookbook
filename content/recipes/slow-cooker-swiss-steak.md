  - "1½ pounds boneless beef round steak, cut into 6"
  - "pieces"
  - "6 – 8 new potatoes, cut into quarters"
  - "1½ cups fresh or frozen whole baby carrots"
  - "1 medium onion, sliced"
  - "1 can (14½ oz.) diced tomatoes with basil, garlic"
  - "and oregano"
  - "1 can (10¼ oz.) Campbell’s® Beef Gravy"
---

Cook beef in 2 batches in large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until browned. Place beef, potatoes, carrots and onion in 3½-quart slow cooker. Mix tomatoes and gravy. Pour over beef and vegetables.

Cover and cook on low for 8 – 10 hours or on high for 4 – 5 hours or until beef is fork tender.
