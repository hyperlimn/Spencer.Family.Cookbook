ingredients:
  - "1 cup Coca Cola® ½ tsp. salt"
  - "½ cup vegetable oil 2 eggs, beaten"
  - "1 stick butter 2 cups all-purpose flour"
  - "3 Tbsp. cocoa ½ cup buttermilk"
  - "2 cups granulated sugar 1 tsp. vanilla extract"
  - "1 tsp. baking soda"
  - "In a saucepan, bring Coca Cola®, oil, butter, and cocoa"
  - "to a boil ; boil for 1/2 minute. Mix together the sugar,"
  - "flour, and salt. Pour in the boiling liquid and"
  - "extract and beat well. Pour into a greased and floured"
  - "sheet cake pan, and bake at 350º for 20 – 25 minutes,"
  - "depending on oven."
  - "Frosting:"
  - "½ cup butter 1 tsp. vanilla extract"
  - "3 Tbsp. cocoa ½-1 cup pecans, chopped"
  - "6 Tbsp. cream or milk 1 lb. confectioners’ sugar"
  - "In a saucepan, combine the butter, cocoa, and milk."
  - "Heat until butter melts. Beat in the remaining"
  - "ingredients and spread on the hot cake. Cool and cut."
  - "Enjoy!"
