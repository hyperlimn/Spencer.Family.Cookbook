Add 3 ¼ cups sugar; mix well. Add 12 eggs, one at a time, heating after each egg. Add flavorings: 1 tsp. anise oil 2 Tbsp. lemon juice 2 Tbsp. vanilla ¼ tsp. cinnamon Add 7 – 8 cups flour, 1 cup at a time.

Bake in pizzelle iron .
