Cover 2 lbs. pinto beans with water ; bring to boil. Boil for 5 minutes;; then drain in colander and rinse in cold water. Put beans in fresh pot of water; cover with lots of water. Add ham bone Salt & pepper to taste Bring to a full boil ; then turn to medium heat and cook slowly for 3 hours. Add more water as necessary to keep it “soupy.” Serve with raw onions and cornbread. (I use Jiffy cornbread mix & add sugar).
