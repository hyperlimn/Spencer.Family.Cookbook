ingredients:
  - "1 pound ground beef"
  - "½ cup shredded Mozzarella cheese"
  - "½ cup fresh bread crumbs"
  - "½ cup Kraft Thick ‘N Spicy Original Barbecue"
  - "Sauce, divided"
  - "4 hamburger buns"
---

Preheat grill to medium-high heat. Mix meat, cheese, bread crumbs, and 2 Tbsp. of the barbecue sauce. Shape into 4 patties.

Place patties on grill’ and cover grill with lid. Grill 4 to 6 minutes on each side or until burgers are cooked through, brushing with remaining 2 Tbsp. barbecue sauce during last 2 minutes of grilling time.

Fill buns with burgers and lettuce, if desired.

Options: Add 2 Tbsp. grated Parmesan cheese and ¼ tsp. Italian seasoning to the meat mixture. Serve with lettuce and pickles.
