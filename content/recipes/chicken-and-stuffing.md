ingredients:
  - "8 – 10 skinless, boneless chicken breasts"
  - "1 can cream of chicken soup"
  - "8 slices Swiss or mozzarella cheese"
  - "1 pkg. Stove Top® stuffing mix for chicken"
  - "1 small onion"
  - "½ cup butter"
  - "Season to taste with any of the following:"
  - "Garlic powder"
  - "Lawry’s seasoning salt"
  - "Black pepper"
  - "Paprika"
---

Place chicken in 9-x 13-inch dish. Season to taste. Stir soup and 1 can of water together; pour over chicken. Place a slice of cheese on each piece of chicken. Mix stuffing according to directions, but add more butter and onion. Using an ice cream scoop, mound stuffing on top of each piece of cheese. Bake, covered, for 1 hour and then uncover and bake 15 minutes more at 350º

My family’s favorite!
