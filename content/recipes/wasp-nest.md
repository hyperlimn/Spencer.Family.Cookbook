1 box “cook and serve” vanilla pudding (not instant) 2 cups milk 1 box yellow cake mix 1 (11 oz.) bag of butterscotch chips

In a 4-quart saucepan, cook pudding mix and milk according to pudding directions. When pudding begins to thicken or bubble, remove from stove. Then add cake mix to pudding and blend well with whisk. Pour into prepared 9-x 13-inch baking pan. Spread evenly over top of cake batter the entire bag of butterscotch chips.

Bake at 350º for 25-28 minutes. Serve warm or cooled. No frosting needed. Delicious!
