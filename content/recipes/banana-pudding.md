ingredients:
  - "1 large box vanilla pudding"
  - "1 (8 oz.) pkg. cream cheese"
  - "1 can Eagle Brand® Sweetened Condensed milk"
  - "1 (12 oz. container) Cool Whip®"
  - "2 pkgs. Vanilla wafers"
  - "8 bananas, sliced"
  - "Milk (for pudding)"
  - "Prepare pudding as directed on package."
---

Blend in cream cheese and Eagle Brand® milk with pudding. Fold in Cool Whip®. Layer cookies, bananas, and pudding, beginning and ending with cookies, in a 9-x 13-inch pan. Chill overnight.
