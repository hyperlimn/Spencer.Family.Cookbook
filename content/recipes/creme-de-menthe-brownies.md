ingredients:
  - "Cake Layer Ingredients:"
  - "½ cup butter, softened"
  - "1 cup sugar"
  - "4 eggs"
  - "1 (16 oz.) can chocolate syrup"
  - "1 tsp. vanilla"
  - "1 cup all-purpose flour"
  - "½ tsp. salt"
  - "Preheat oven to 350º. Cream butter and sugar in large"
  - "mixing bowl at medium speed until light and fluffy. Beat"
  - "eggs, syrup, and vanilla. Add flour and salt; mix at low"
  - "speed until blended. Spread batter into a greased"
  - "9-x 13-inch pan. Bake for 20 – 25 minutes or until"
  - "a wooden toothpick comes out clean. Cool on rack."
  - "Second Layer Ingredients:"
  - "2 cups confectioners’ sugar"
  - "½ cup butter, softened"
  - "32 Tbsp. crème de menthe"
  - "Combine confectioners’ sugar, butter, and crème de menthe"
  - "in a small bowl at low speed. Spread over cooled brownie"
  - "layer."
