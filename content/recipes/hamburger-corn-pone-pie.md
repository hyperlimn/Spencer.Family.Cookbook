ingredients:
  - "1 lb. ground beef"
  - "1/3 cup chopped onions"
  - "2 tsp. chili powder"
  - "1 tsp. salt"
  - "1 cup tomato juice ( I add a little more for moisture)"
  - "1 tsp. Worcestershire sauce"
  - "1 cup cooked macaroni (drained)"
  - "1 can fancy red kidney beans (drained)"
  - "1 box Jiffy® corn bread mix"
---

Add a little sugar

Brown hamburger with onions, salt, & chili powder. Add tomato juice, beans, and macaroni. Pour into baking dish. Make corn bread according to package directions. ( I add a little sugar.) Pour corn bread mix over meat mixture. Bake uncovered until corn bread is lightly browned on top at 400º. Make sure corn bread is done all the way through.
