Heat 1 Tbsp. oil in large skillet on medium heat. Add onions; cook 20 to 25 minutes or until lightly browned, stirring occasionally. Stir in 1½ tsp. sugar; cook 5 minutes or until golden brown, stirring occasionally.

Drizzle with 2 Tbsp. Kraft® Special Collection Balsamic Vinaigrette Dressing just before serving.

Refrigerate in a tightly covered container up to 4 days.
