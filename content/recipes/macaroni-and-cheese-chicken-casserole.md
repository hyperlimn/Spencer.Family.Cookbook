Add 1 cup cooked chicken breast, cubed (or a can of chicken breast, drained) (may also use cubed ham or turkey) 1 can cream of chicken soup ½ soup can of milk

Mix ingredients well and place in casserole dish. Crumble Ritz® crackers on top and dot with pats of butter. Bake at 350º for 30 minutes.

Great for leftover ham or turkey.
