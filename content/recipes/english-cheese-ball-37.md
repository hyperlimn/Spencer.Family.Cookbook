ingredients:
  - "16 oz. cream cheese, softened"
  - "1 jar Old English cheese spread"
  - "3/4-1 cup walnuts or pecans, chopped"
---

Mix cream cheese and Old English cheese in mixer until well blended. Chill for 2 hours. Form into 2 balls. Roll in nuts. Serve with pretzel crackers.
