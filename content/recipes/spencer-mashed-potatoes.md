Using electric mixer, mash potatoes to get them to a chunky consistency. Add Carnation® Evaporated milk and 1 stick butter. (Depending on the type and amount of potatoes you may have to add a second can of milk or you may add regular milk and more butter.) Mash until potatoes are creamy and fluffy. Place pats of butter on top of potatoes once you have placed them in your serving dish.

Unbelievably delicious!
