ingredients:
  - "2 1/3 cups Bisquick®"
  - "3½ cups milk"
  - "3 Tbsp. sugar"
  - "3 Tbsp. melted butter"
  - "1 quart strawberries, sliced & sweetened"
---

Heat oven to 425º. Stir all ingredients except strawberries until soft dough forms. Drop by spoonful onto ungreased baking sheet. Bake 10 – 12 minutes. Split; fill and top with strawberries and whipped cream, if desired.
