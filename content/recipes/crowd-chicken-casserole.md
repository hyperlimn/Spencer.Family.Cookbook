ingredients:
  - "10 cups diced cooked chicken"
  - "10 cups chopped celery"
  - "2 bunches green onions with tops, sliced"
  - "2 cans (4 oz. each ) chopped green chilies"
  - "1 can (5¾ oz.) pitted ripe olives, drained & sliced"
  - "(optional)"
  - "2 cups slivered almonds"
  - "5 cups (20 oz.) shredded cheddar cheese, divided"
  - "2 cups mayonnaise"
  - "2 cups (16 oz.) sour cream"
  - "5 cups crushed potato chips"
---

Combine the first 6 ingredients. Add 2 cups cheese. Mix mayonnaise and sour cream; add to chicken mixture and toss. Spoon into two greased 9-x 13-inch baking dishes. Sprinkle with chips. Top with remaining cheese. Bake, uncovered, at 350º for 20 – 25 minutes or until hot. Yield: 24 servings.
