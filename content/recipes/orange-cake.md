Mix together following ingredients and beat: 1 box yellow cake mix 3eggs ¾ cup oil 1 (11 oz.) can mandarin oranges and juice

Pour into 9-x 13-inch greased, floured pan. Bake at 350º for 25 – 30 minutes. Cool.

Icing:

1 (20 oz.) can crushed pineapple, drained 1 tsp. vanilla 1 (3 oz.) pkg. instant coconut pudding mix, dry ½ tsp. lemon juice (optional) 1 (9 oz. container) Cool Whip®

Combine first 4 ingredients and then fold in Cool Whip®. cake. Refrigerate.
