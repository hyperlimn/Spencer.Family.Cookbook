ingredients:
  - "2 lbs. ground beef"
  - "1 can (28 oz.) tomato sauce"
  - "1 can (28 oz.) diced tomatoes, undrained"
  - "2 cups cooked rice (not Minute Rice) (add when"
  - "you serve or it absorbs liquid)"
  - "2 cups chopped green pepper"
  - "2 beef bouillon cubes"
  - "¼ cup packed brown sugar (can use Splenda/"
  - "smaller amount)"
  - "2 tsp. Salt"
  - "1 tsp. Pepper"
---

Brown ground beef, drain. Add all ingredients; bring to boil. Reduce heat. Cover and simmer for 30 – 40 minutes. Or you can place in slow cooker and cook for 4 hours on low.

Note: 2/3 cup dry rice and 1 1/3 cups water = 2 cups of cooked rice.
