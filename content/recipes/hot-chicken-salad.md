ingredients:
  - "2 cups cooked chicken"
  - "2 cups coarsely chopped celery"
  - "½ cup chopped, toasted almonds"
  - "1 cup mayonnaise (add a little more to make"
  - "more moist)"
  - "2 tsp. grated onion"
  - "½ tsp. salt"
  - "2 tsp. lemon juice"
  - "1 cup crushed potato chips"
  - "½ cup grated Cheddar cheese"
---

Combine chicken, celery, and almonds with mayonnaise. Mix together onion, salt, and lemon juice; add to chicken. Place in 2-quart casserole dish. Top with potato chips and Cheddar cheese. Bake at 450º for about 20 minutes. Serves 6.

(May be made ahead of time — except put topping on just before placing in oven.)
