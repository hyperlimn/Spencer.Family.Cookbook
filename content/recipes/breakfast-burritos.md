ingredients:
  - "1 pkg. Jimmy Dean sausage"
  - "1 small onion, chopped"
  - "1 large potato, baked, peeled, diced"
  - "6 eggs, beaten with fork"
  - "6 flour tortillas, heated"
---

Brown sausage and onion in skillet. Drain. Add diced potato.

In a separate skillet, scramble the eggs. Add to the sausage mixture. Place generous amount of cooked mixture into a steamed tortilla. Excellent served with picante sauce or salsa
