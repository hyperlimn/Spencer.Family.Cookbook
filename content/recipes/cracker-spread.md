ingredients:
  - "2 large pkgs. cream cheese"
  - "½ cup Miracle Whip®"
  - "1/3 cup Parmesan cheese"
  - "¼ cup green onion, chopped"
  - "10 slices crisp bacon, crumbled"
---

Cook bacon until crisp. Mix all other ingredients and stir in bacon when cool and crumbled. Spread on crackers.
