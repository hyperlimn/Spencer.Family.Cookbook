ingredients:
  - "6 medium-sized green or red peppers"
  - "1 lb. ground beef"
  - "¼ cup rice, cooked"
  - "1 can tomato soup"
  - "1 soup can of milk"
  - "Ketchup or tomato sauce"
  - "Salt & pepper to taste"
  - "Grated onion"
---

Cut tops off peppers & remove seeds. Cook rice. Mix all ingredients together. Add ketchup or tomato sauce to meat mixture. Stuff peppers with meat mixture. Heat tomato soup mixed with milk. Pour diluted soup over peppers. Sprinkle bread crumbs on top of meat. Bake covered at 350º for 1½ hours . Top each with a slice of American cheese.

Rice (1 cup) ½ cup water ¼ cup rice
