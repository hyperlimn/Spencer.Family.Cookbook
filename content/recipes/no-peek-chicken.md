ingredients:
  - "1 small box Uncle Ben’s® Long Grain"
  - "and Wild Rice"
  - "1 can cream of mushroom soup"
  - "1 can cream of celery soup"
  - "1 can cold water"
  - "1 tsp. parsley"
  - "Dash of curry powder (optional)"
  - "6 to 8 chicken breasts )"
  - "1 pkg. Lipton® onion soup mix"
---

Lightly grease 9-x 13-inch dish. Mix first 6 ingredients and place in pan. Lay chicken on top and sprinkle with the Lipton® soup mix. Cover and seal with foil. Bake at 350° for 2½ hours. (Don’t peek!)
