ingredients:
  - "1 package Stove Top® Stuffing Mix for Chicken"
  - "1½ pounds boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut"
  - "into 1-inch pieces"
  - "1 bag (14 oz.) frozen broccoli florets, thawed,"
  - "drained"
  - "1 can (10¾ oz.) condensed cream of chicken soup"
  - "½ cup milk"
  - "1½ cups shredded cheddar cheese"
---

Preheat oven to 400º. Prepare stuffing mix as directed on package; set aside. Mix chicken and broccoli in 9-x 13-inch baking dish. Stir in soup, milk, and cheese. Top with prepared stuffing. Bake 30 minutes or until cooked through.
