ingredients:
  - "1 (16 oz.) can refried beans"
  - "1 (8 oz.) pkg. cream cheese, softened"
  - "1 cup sour cream"
  - "2 Tbsp. Taco seasoning mix"
  - "2 garlic cloves, pressed"
  - "2 oz. cheddar cheese, shredded"
  - "½ cup pitted, ripe olives, chopped"
  - "2 Tbsp. Fresh cilantro or parsley, snipped"
  - "1 medium tomato, seeded and chopped"
  - "¼ cup thinly sliced green onions with tops"
---

Preheat oven to 350º. Spread beans over bottom of deep dish baker using a scraper. In a 2-quart bowl combine cream cheese, sour cream, and taco season- ing. Press garlic into bowl using a garlic press; mix well. Spread over beans. Shred cheese over top and bake for 15-18 minutes or until hot. Chop olives and snip cilantro. Sprinkle tomato, onions, olives, and cilantro over dip. Garnish with additional sour cream, if desired. Serve with tortilla chips or freshly cut vegetables.
