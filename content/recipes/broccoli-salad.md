ingredients:
  - "1 small onion"
  - "1 lb. thickly sliced bacon, peppered, cooked"
  - "2 heads broccoli, raw"
  - "1 cup Hellman’s® mayonnaise"
  - "½ cup sugar"
  - "2 Tbsp. vinegar"
---

Cut broccoli into pieces. Cook bacon. Mix remaining ingredients and pour over. Marinate in refrigerator. Serve cold.
