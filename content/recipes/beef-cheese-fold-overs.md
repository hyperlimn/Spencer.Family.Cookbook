ingredients:
  - "1 lb. Ground beef"
  - "¼ cup onion, chopped"
  - "¾ cup barbecue sauce"
  - "6 slices American cheese"
  - "2 (8oz.) cans refrigerated crescent rolls"
  - "2 tsp. Toasted sesame seeds"
---

Brown meat & onion; drain. Stir in barbecue sauce, set aside. Heat oven to 375º. Unroll crescent rolls into 4 long rectangles on ungreased cookie sheet. Overlap long sides; firmly press perforations & edges to seal. Pat to form 15 x 13 - inch rectangle. Spread meat mixture lengthwise in a 4-inch wide strip down center of dough to within 1 inch of ends. Top meat with cheese slices. Fold shorter sides of dough 1 inch over filling. Bring long sides of dough rectangle over filling, overlapping edges. Pinch edge to seal. Sprinkle with sesame seeds. Bake at 375º for 20 – 25 minutes or until golden brown. Let stand 5 minutes before serving.
