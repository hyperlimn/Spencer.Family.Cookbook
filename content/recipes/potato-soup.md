Cut into 2-inch cubes Small onion, diced 3 stalks celery, diced Cover with cold water about 1-2 inches above the ingredients. Bring to a boil and boil for about 30 minutes or until potatoes and celery are tender. Turn down to low and add a stick of butter ; let it melt. Add 1 can of Carnation evaporated milk. Stir and simmer for about 30 minutes. Add shredded cheese, bacon bits, and scallions when serving, if desired.
