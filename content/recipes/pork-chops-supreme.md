Heat oven to 350º.

Trim excess fat from 2 lean pork chops ( 1-inch thick).

Place chops in baking pan or dish. Salt well.

Top each chop with a lemon slice (or use lemon juice) and a thin onion slice.

Sprinkle generously with brown sugar.

Pour 1 Tbsp. ketchup over each chop.

Cover and bake 1 hour.

Uncover and bake 30 minutes longer, basting occasionally. Tastes like barbequed pork chops.
