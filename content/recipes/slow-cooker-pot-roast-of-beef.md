Slow Cooker Orange

Chicken with Green Onions

and Walnuts

1½ cups chicken broth ¼ cup teriyaki sauce 3 cloves garlic, minced ¾ cup orange marmalade 4 green onions, sliced (about ½ cup) 2 Tbsp. cornstarch 8 skinless, boneless chicken breasts ½ cup walnut pieces Hot cooked rice

Mix broth, teriyaki sauce, garlic, marmalade, ¼ cup green onions and cornstarch in 6-quart slow cooker. Add chicken and turn to coat.

Cover and cook on low 8 – 9 hours or on high for 4 – 5 hours, until chicken is no longer pink. Sprinkle with walnuts and remaining green onions before serving. Serve with rice.
