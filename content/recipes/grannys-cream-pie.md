Stir well and cook until thick.

Add: 2 Tbsp. butter 2 tsp. vanilla Pour into baked pie shell. Top with meringue or whipped topping.

Note: To make chocolate cream pie, add ½ cup Hershey’s® cocoa. Top with whipped topping or meringue.

To make coconut pie, add 1 cup flaked coconut. Top with whipped topping or meringue and garnish with toasted coconut flakes.
