ingredients:
  - "½ cup butter or margarine"
  - "1 cup crushed graham crackers crumbs"
  - "1 cup coconut"
  - "1 cup chocolate morsels"
  - "1 cup chopped nuts"
  - "1 can sweetened condensed milk"
---

Melt butter and stir in crumbs. Press into the bottom of pan. Combine other ingredients and pour into pan. Bake 25 – 30 minutes at 350º. Let cool. Cut into small squares.

Use 9-inch square pan or double the recipe and put in a 9-x 13-inch pan.
