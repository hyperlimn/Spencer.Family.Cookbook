Preheat oven to 350º. Grease and flour or grease and sugar 2 loaf pans. 4 cups flour 2 tsp. ground cloves 2 tsp. cinnamon 1 tsp. salt 2 tsp. baking soda 1 tsp. nutmeg Step 1: Combine dry ingredients in bowl and stir together. 1 cup brown sugar 1 cup oil 2 cups white sugar Step 2: In separate bowl, combine oil and sugars with a hand mixer. Step 3: Add 2 eggs to the sugar and oil mixture and beat well. Step 4: Add alternately to the sugar and oil mixture, a little flour mixture and a little buttermilk, then mix with mixer, then add some more flour and more buttermilk until gone. You will use 2 cups of buttermilk total. Step 5: Pour into 2 bread pans and bake for 1 hour or until cake tester comes out clean. Let cool for 10 minutes; then remove from pans onto a cooling rack. When cool, wrap in aluminum foil. Keeps 1 month in Tupperware® in refrigerator or freezes well in foil.
