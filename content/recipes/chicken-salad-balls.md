ingredients:
  - "1 cup cooked chicken, chopped"
  - "1 Tbsp. onion, chopped"
  - "2 Tbsp. pimento, chopped"
  - "Dash hot sauce"
  - "½ cup mayonnaise"
  - "1 cup pecans, chopped"
---

Combine all ingredients. Mix well. Chill several hours. Shape into 1-inch balls.
