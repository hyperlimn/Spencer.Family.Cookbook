Mix together: 4 eggs, beaten with fork 1 cup flaked coconut 2 sticks butter, melted 2 tsp. baking powder 2 cups white sugar 1 tsp. Vanilla 1 cup Carnation® evaporated milk Add 2 cups mashed sweet potatoes (I use 1 pkg. frozen yam patties. They are usually in the frozen section & sometimes with the meats. Canned yams do not taste the same.) Pour into large, deep dish casserole, greased with butter . Topping: Mix together: 1 cup brown sugar ½ tsp. salt ½ cup flour ½ tsp. baking powder 1 cup pecans Put topping on right before you bake. Dot with ½ stick butter sliced into pats. (Sometimes I double the topping recipe.) Bake uncovered at 350º for approximately 1 hour. Top will be firm and crunchy looking. Insert knife to check — should not be very “gooey.” (This can be made the night before. Make the topping and place it in a Ziploc® baggie. Do not put topping on until just before you bake. Let casserole get to room temperature before baking or allow longer to bake.) Fantastic!
