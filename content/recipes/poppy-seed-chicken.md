ingredients:
  - "6 to 8 chicken breasts, boiled and cut up"
  - "1 cup sour cream"
  - "2 cans of Campbell’s® Cream of Chicken & Mushroom"
  - "soup"
---

Mix the 2 cans of soup, sour cream, and chicken Together, and set aside.

2 sleeves Ritz® crackers, blended into crumbs in a food processor 1/2 jar poppy seeds 1 stick of melted butter

Mix cracker crumbs, poppy seeds, and butter until well blended.

Place the chicken mixture in a slow cooker; cover with crumbs and cook on low for 5 to 6 hours.

Note: To bake in the oven, place half the cracker crumbs on bottom of rectangular pan. Place all of the chicken mixture on top of that. Then put the rest of the cracker crumbs on top. Bake at 350º for 35 minutes.
