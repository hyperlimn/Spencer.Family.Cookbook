ingredients:
  - "Combine:"
  - "2 egg whites, beaten"
  - "¾ cups sugar"
  - "1/3 cup white Karo® syrup"
  - "2 Tbsp. water"
  - "¼ tsp. cream of tartar (can omit)"
  - "¼ tsp. salt"
---

Combine sugar, Karo® syrup, and water. Let come to boil and thicken. It is done when it is stringy from a spoon).

Slowly pour ½ of the syrup into beaten egg whites. Cook remaining syrup for 2 minutes. Add rest of syrup. Beat well (until it loses its gloss). Spread on cake.

If consistency is not right, add powdered sugar. Keep refrigerated. Best on chocolate cake!
