ingredients:
  - "1 ¼ cup graham cracker crumbs 1½ Tbsp. lemon juice"
  - "¼ cup butter, melted ¾ tsp. Vanilla"
  - "11 oz. cream cheese, softened Dash of salt"
  - "¾ cup sugar 3 eggs"
  - "1 ½ cups sour cream"
  - "3 Tbsp. sugar"
  - "¾ tsp. Vanilla"
---

Mix crumbs and butter in deep pie plate. Beat cream cheese. Blend in sugar, lemon juice, vanilla, and salt. Add eggs, one at a time. Pour into crust. Bake at 325º for 25-30 minutes. (Center will not be firm.) Remove from oven.

Meanwhile, mix together:

Spoon sour cream mixture on top of pie, beginning from outside edges and working toward the center of pie plate. Bake 10-20 minutes more (until it loses its gloss. Cool and then refrigerate, covered.
