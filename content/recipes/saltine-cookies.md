ingredients:
  - "35 + saltine crackers"
  - "2 sticks butter"
  - "1 cup dark brown sugar"
  - "3/4 of 12 oz. pkg. semisweet chocolate chips"
  - "Chopped nuts, optional"
  - "Pam cooking spray"
---

Line large jelly roll pan with foil. Grease the foil. (Can use Pam)

Lay saltines on pan with sides touching.

Melt butter in saucepan. Add brown sugar. Cook gently until bubbling and brown looking all over. Pour over saltines and spread with spatula. Bake at 350º for 5 minutes.

Meanwhile, melt chocolate chips so they are spreadable. Spread chocolate over hot or warm saltines evenly with spatula. Sprinkle nuts on top, if desired. Cool 1 hour in refrigerator. Pick up foil; peel foil away. Cut from caramel side. Yummy!
