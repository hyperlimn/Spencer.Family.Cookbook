ingredients:
  - "2 cups sour cream"
  - "¾ cup sugar"
  - "1 Tbsp. + 1 tsp. lemon juice"
  - "1 (30 oz.) can fruit cocktail"
  - "2 medium bananas, cut into ¼-inch pieces"
  - "½ cup chopped nuts"
  - "1 jar maraschino cherries, drained & halved"
---

Mix sour cream, sugar, & lemon juice. Stir in fruit, bananas, nuts, & cherries. Place into 2 ice trays or Pyrex® dish. Freeze for 24 hours. About 15 minutes before serving, place in refrigerator.
