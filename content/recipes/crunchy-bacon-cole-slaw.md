  - "1 cup shredded red cabbage"
  - "½ cup chopped peanuts, optional"
  - "4 slices bacon, crisply cooked & crumbled"

Mix Miracle Whip® and sugar in large bowl. Add cabbage, peanuts, and bacon; toss. Refrigerate.
