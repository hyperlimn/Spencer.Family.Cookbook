ingredients:
  - "1 lb. corkscrew pasta, cooked"
  - "Cucumbers, diced"
  - "1 bundle green onions"
  - "1 red pepper"
  - "Cubed cheddar & provolone cheese"
  - "Sliced pepperoni"
  - "1 jar green olives"
  - "1 small can black olives"
  - "1 regular size Wishbone® Zesty Italian dressing"
  - "1 small bottle Salad Supreme (Use ¾ bottle.)"
---

Cook pasta according to directions. Drain. Rinse in cold water. Add remaining ingredients to cooled pasta. Sprinkle Salad Supreme® over salad. Pour Italian dressing over pasta. Refrigerate.
