ingredients:
  - "3 lbs. ground meat ½ tsp. garlic powder"
  - "1 can evaporated milk ½ cup chopped onions"
  - "1 cup quick oats 2 tsp. salt"
  - "1 cup cracker crumbs ½ tsp. pepper"
  - "2 eggs 2 tsp. chili powder"
  - "2 cups ketchup"
  - "1 cup brown sugar"
  - "¼ cup chopped onions"
  - "½ tsp. liquid smoke ( I leave this out.)"
---

Mix together & shape into balls; place on wax paper. Place into freezer bags and freeze. When needed, bake at 350º for 1 hour.

SAUCE

Mix together above ingredients. Heat on stovetop at medium temperature until thoroughly heated. Pour over meatballs. Serve hot.
