  - "¼ cup Kraft Zesty Italian Dressing"
  - "1 Tbsp. soy sauce"
  - "2 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "Your choice of meat:"
  - "1 pound boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into strips"
  - "OR 1 pound boneless beef sirloin steak, cut into strips"
  - "OR 1 pound boneless pork chops, cut into strips"
  - "Vegetables:"
  - "1 each: red, green and yellow pepper, chopped"
  - "2 green onions, sliced"
  - "Directions:"
  - "1. Mix sauce ingredients. Add 1 Tbsp. of the sauce mix-"
  - "ture to the meat; toss to coat. Let stand 5 minutes."
  - "2. Heat oil in large nonstick wok or skillet on"
  - "medium-high heat. Add meat mixture and"
  - "vegetables; stir-fry 4 minutes or until meat is cooked"
  - "through."
  - "3. Add remaining sauce mixture; simmer 2"
  - "minutes. Serve over rice. Makes 4 servings."
