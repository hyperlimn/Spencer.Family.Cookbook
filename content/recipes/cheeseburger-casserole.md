1 lb. ground beef ¼ cup ketchup ¼ cup chopped onion 8 oz. can tomato sauce 1 tube refrigerated rolls

Brown ground beef with chopped onions and salt & pepper. Add tomato sauce and ketchup and allow to simmer. Remove from heat. In casserole put about 1/3 of hamburger mixture and 4 slices cheese. Alternate in layers with ground beef on top layer. Lay 10 rolls from a refrigerated tube on top and bake at 350º for 10 minutes or until rolls are golden brown and cooked through.
