ingredients:
  - "2 cups Hellman’s® mayonnaise"
  - "1 heaping Tbsp. prepared yellow mustard"
  - "1/8 cup sugar"
  - "Mix together and place in refrigerator until needed."
  - "8 hard boiled eggs, peeled and cut"
  - "1 large Vidalia or sweet white onion, chopped"
  - "½ green pepper, chopped"
  - "4 small sweet gherkin pickles, chopped"
  - "½ carrot, shredded"
  - "Put all prepared items into a bowl and set aside. This"
  - "can be done while potatoes are boiling."
  - "Potatoes:"
  - "Scrub 3-4 pounds of redskin potatoes (new potatoes)."
  - "Place into large pan and boil (with skins) for one hour."
  - "Peel and cut into pieces, while potatoes are hot , into a"
  - "large bowl. (Use rubber gloves to handle hot potatoes.)"
  - "Add chopped ingredients to the cut potatoes. Sprinkle"
  - "with salt and pepper to taste. Add dressing and gently"
  - "stir/toss. Refrigerate. Best when served the day it is"
  - "made."
