ingredients:
  - "1 can cream of celery soup"
  - "1 (8 oz.) pkg. Cream cheese"
  - "½ lb. Sandwich salami (or pepperoni), diced"
  - "French bread"
---

Heat soup (undiluted). Add cream cheese and melt until creamy. Add pepperoni. Simmer until heated through. Cut bread in ½ inch slices. Dip bread into mixture.
