Fried eggs in butter, leave yolk runny. Salt and Pepper to taste.  
Toast bread 
Spread Mayonnaise on toast
Sprinkle Tony Chacherd’s seasoning over mayonnaise.
Place slice tomatoes on bread 
Top with fried egg 

Eat with a fork or pick up like a 1/2 sandwich. You can mix and match and add avocado or bacon as well!

## Family note

Liz made it up with friends and the name Tom Toms stuck for no reason other than the tomatoes.
