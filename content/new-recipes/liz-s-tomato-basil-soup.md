Cut tomatoes in half and fill a cookie sheet with tomatoes and 5 or 6 garlic cloves, with some olive oil drizzled over them. Put in oven at 400° until softened.
Sometimes we broil at 500 towards the end to slightly brown the skin of tomatoes.

While tomatoes are in the oven, sauté the sliced onions in the 3/4 stick of butter.

Once tomatoes are done pour them, the garlic, and juices into blender , pour sautéed onions butter and the basil into blender .
Blend smooth.

Pour into pot and add some broth and some heavy cream.
